# Climb batch 29: orientation admission

Eight entries are admitted. They make four edits, all in the orientation's numeral rules:

1. **A numeral converts where it stands (P-173, F-341, F-342, F-79).** This rewrites the rule "A numeral has a type of its own" in "Numbers" (`:89`), and renames `IntLiteral` at `:95`.
2. **An expression of numerals alone folds exactly before the checker, on both paths (P-173, F-339, F-343).** This is a new bold rule in "Numbers", after edit 1.
3. **A numeral at `Index` takes its default (F-278, F-342).** This corrects "An indexed type's index parameter is bounded by `Index`" in "Traits and objects" (`:48`).
4. **The departures that the batch closed are deleted (F-343, F-344).** This deletes sentences at `:48`, `:62` and `:89` that cite closed rows (364, 721, 943, 944, 945), and replaces the `:62` example that the fold now makes a numeral.

The batch, the switch-over's literals, makes the orientation's numeral text false in most of its sentences. RECORD.md, "Left for the coordinator and the skill writer", names `revival-changes.md:48`, `:62`, `:89` and `:95`, and the rung reports confirm each (`compile-ladder/rung-numeral-checker/REPORT.md:160`; `compile-ladder/rung-numeral-types/REPORT.md:116`). The defaults that these four edits correct come from three sources: the orientation's own text, the pretraining quiz (literal types that stay polymorphic, overflow that always raises), and the team's tests. The mistakes are on record. Seven tests meant fixed-width or computed arithmetic on numerals alone, which the fold now does exactly, and had to be respelled. Three revival tests now pass by the string fold without checking `||`, a false green. Regression cases 16 and 28 now have wrong keys.

Every other entry either changed only its line citations (32 entries), or is a measure, a native binding or a compiled-path detail that an area part owns. Several area parts and the core now hold false sentences. They are listed under "Elsewhere".
