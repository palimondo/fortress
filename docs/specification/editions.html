The Fortress Language Specification

The editions

The specification at its milestones, oldest first. The Fortress team wrote the first four. The last entry, set apart, is not the team’s.

  1. 19 September 2006 Version 1.0 α

    411 pages. Sun Microsystems published it as a PDF, before the code was opened: the repository’s history starts on 4 January 2007. Only the PDF is known to survive in public; its LaTeX source has not been found.

    PDF, 2.4 MB. This copy was fetched on 10 November 2026 from the University of Jena; its title page carries “© Sun Microsystems, Inc.”, and the file holds no licence statement (where it came from).

  2. 6 March 2007 Version 1.0 β

    441 pages, by the same eight authors. Only the PDF is known to survive in public; its LaTeX source has not been found.

    PDF, 2.0 MB. This copy was fetched on 10 November 2026 from UCLA; its title page carries “© Sun Microsystems, Inc.”, and the file holds no licence statement (where it came from).

  3. 31 March 2008 Version 1.0

    262 pages. The PDF that the team released with the interpreter; it entered the repository on that day, and the tag 1.0 holds it. Its LaTeX source has not been found.

    PDF, 1.2 MB (Specification-1.0-frozen/fortress.1.0.pdf in the repository).

  4. 2 February 2011 The Working Draft

    The specification as the team left it. Its LaTeX entered the repository on 20 October and 5 November 2009, and the team’s last commit of the draft is of 2 February 2011 (2f8b4331a). Its appendix of the changes since 1.0 is a stub that the team left.

    This HTML edition, converted from that LaTeX (Specification-1.0-frozen/, byte for byte the draft of that day). The repository holds no PDF of this draft.

Not the team’s work

  1. 2026 The revised edition of the Fortress revival

    The Fortress revival, which works to finish the team’s design, revises the Working Draft in Specification/. A passage that it changed carries a callout, and its Appendix I lists each change with the original text. It changes the text of 28 of the 54 chapter pages. Its pages ask search engines not to index them.

    The revised edition; its PDF in the repository, 2.5 MB, a draft build.

This page is the edition’s, not the authors’. Its dates and facts are from the repository: Specification-history/README.md, the git history, and the PDFs themselves.
