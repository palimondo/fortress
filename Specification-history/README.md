# Earlier editions of the Fortress Language Specification

Two editions that Sun Microsystems published before version 1.0, kept here as the documents they are. Only their PDFs are known to survive in public; their LaTeX sources have not been found.

- `fortress.1.0alpha.pdf`: *The Fortress Language Specification, Version 1.0 α*, 19 September 2006, 411 pages. Fetched on 2026-11-10 from https://psc.informatik.uni-jena.de/languages/HPCS/fortress-1.0alpha.pdf. sha256 `93d7edd59a12ac01d68409d310d6cdd5cebeae3a2d9f1f7d09d7932182b0e24b`.
- `fortress.1.0beta.pdf`: *The Fortress Language Specification, Version 1.0 β*, 6 March 2007, 441 pages. Fetched on 2026-11-10 from https://web.cs.ucla.edu/~palsberg/course/cs239/papers/fortress1.0beta.pdf. sha256 `8f275267c3992a90a6925d5d5100a5d8e0b656e910a79caccc3d43424d751ead`.

Both name as authors Eric Allen, David Chase, Joe Hallett, Victor Luchangco, Jan-Willem Maessen, Sukyoung Ryu, Guy L. Steele Jr. and Sam Tobin-Hochstadt, with Joao Dias, Carl Eastlund, Christine Flood, Yossi Lev and Cheryl McCosh as additional contributors. Each title page carries "© Sun Microsystems, Inc." and its date; neither file holds a licence statement. They are included at the repository owner's decision of 2026-11-10, unchanged, for the record of how the language's design moved.

The later editions are elsewhere in this repository:

- Version 1.0, 31 March 2008: `Specification-1.0-frozen/fortress.1.0.pdf`, the PDF that the team released with the interpreter (the repository's tag `1.0`).
- The working draft as the team left it, 2 February 2011: the LaTeX sources in `Specification-1.0-frozen/`.
- The revival's revision of that draft: `Specification/`.
