## 0. How to read this

**Sources.**
- Git: `git log -S'<phrase>'` over the tree for each phrase, per file, to find the commit that first wrote a rule in a place and each later commit that changed its wording there; the commit messages; `git show` for the diffs. Dates are the commits' author dates, UTC.

A citation reads "S2 p001 L11119, 2026-09-27T01:18:50Z": the part, the line of the JSONL file, the record's timestamp. Workers' transcripts are the `subagents/agent-<id>.jsonl` files beside them. A line that is a tool call and not a text record is marked so (the briefs to workers are Agent calls; S2 p003 L2730 is the brief of the coordinator skill's writer).
- The curator's messages are the `user` records and the queued messages. He dictates; the speech-to-text slips are left as they are. A queued message is stamped when he sent it and the `user` record when the session took it, so the two can differ by minutes; the `user` record's time is given.

**Models.** Named by tier and version, read from the `model` field of the assistant records of the main thread (or of the worker's own transcript where it is at hand). The coordinating session ran on Fable 5 from the start (2026-08-18T21:03) to 2026-09-08T16:08; on Fable 5.1 from 2026-09-08T16:15 to 2026-09-25T22:48, except two spells on Opus 5 (S1 2026-09-17T11:51 to the end of its backup at 20:31, and S2 2026-09-18T09:50 to 18:16); on Opus 5.5 from 2026-09-25T22:58 to 2026-10-11T06:47 (with 51 Fable 5.1 records on 2026-10-02 10:00 to 10:12); on Fable 5.1 again from 2026-10-11T06:50. Every copy below was written while the model named for it was the coordinating one; where a worker wrote or drafted the text, the worker is named with its model.

**The classes of trigger.** For each copy the event just before it is classed so:
- *failure*: the coordinator broke the rule (or the thing it covers) and he, or the coordinator itself, said so shortly before the copy was written;
- *request*: he asked for this record, rule or rewrite, with no failure of that rule just before;
- *move*: the text was carried into the place by a rewrite, consolidation, split or skill extraction, set going by a request about the whole file or skill, not about the rule;
- *none found*.
A copy that has a failure and a request together is classed by the event the coordinator itself named. Which class a copy belongs to is my reading of the quotes; the quotes are given so that it can be re-read.

**What counts as a copy.** The commit that wrote the rule in one of the places counted in section 4.1, or that rewrote it there in a way that changed its words. A commit that only moved the line, or changed other words of the same line, is not counted.

**Limits.** The searches for failures are regular expressions over the curator's messages and the coordinator's text ("again", "already decided", "I told you", "clock", "guess", "delegate", "yesterday" and the like), read in context; a failure he did not name in such words may be missing. The line numbers are JSONL lines, not paragraph numbers.
