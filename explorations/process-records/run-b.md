## Findings

The run's first six minutes are a single wide read — ledger, four reviews, the retrospective, three prior programs, the matrix-AD report, the blinded run's tooling and notes — and the shape of everything after it follows from that read: the goldens were cross-checked against the blinded run's within eight minutes of starting, the article's tooling is the blinded run's tooling adapted, and the departures table cites ledger rows instead of re-deriving them.

The brief fixed the flagship design in advance, so the run's own design work is narrow and measured: two skeletons, a depth measurement that decided between them by numbers rather than argument, and a third shape declared sketched-not-built; the decision paragraph in §4.6 names what each contributed to the engine that shipped.

Most of the run's probe effort went into a small number of blocked routes rather than a broad survey — pasting (`c02` through `c02f`, six files), the `^T` overload pair (`c09`, `c10`), the parameter record (`c18`), the `‖` continuation (`c17`) — and each blocked route left both a workaround in the program and a numbered row in `gaps.md`.

The two corrections the worker returned both landed on claims the run had already written down: one where it had read an interpreter answer as meaning more than it did (`===` on a `value object`), and one where it had concluded a library constant was absent (`pi`); the worker also found a legal route to the `^T` overloads the run had ruled out for its own carriers, which arrived after the engine was built and is recorded in row 84 and §4.6 rather than acted on.

Delegation was used once, late, and only for the gate that asks for a second pair of eyes; the parallelism was real (the worker's 24 minutes overlap the article work almost exactly) and the reconciliation cost the last eight minutes of the run.

Time was lost twice: about five minutes at 01:13–01:22 rebuilding both skeletons after the `^T` and pasting routes failed, and eight minutes at 02:10–02:18 idle at the end, where the run had finished everything except the worker's verdicts and waited.

The by-eye discipline the brief demanded shows up as inspection loops rather than assertions: figure sheets were rendered and read at 01:05, 01:09, 01:42, 01:48 and 01:54, and the built page was read as sliced screenshots in light, dark and narrow form before it was committed.

Against the prior work, the run neither ignored it nor inherited from it silently: the earlier scalar programs are reproduced as figures inside its own article (`figures/prior_*`) for the pairs where it judges the scalar form reads better, and §4.7 says so line by line.

The result — a 396-line core, 54 passing checks at one and four threads, a 24-row departures table, 25 new ledger rows, an independent reproduction of eighteen of them, and a 3.4 MB page — was produced in 91 minutes of wall time with one human sentence of input, at 1.8 million output tokens on the main thread and 36,000 on the worker.
