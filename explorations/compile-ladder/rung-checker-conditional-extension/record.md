## Revival change

**The change.** The team's library declared its list type lexicographically ordered at every element type, `trait List[\E\] extends { AnyList, LexicographicOrder[\List[\E\],E\] }` with `trait LexicographicOrder[\T extends LexicographicOrder[\T,E\],E\]` unbounded (`Library/List.fsi:67`, `Library/FortressLibrary.fsi:1196`, `Library/FortressLibrary.fss:1735` at `a874948ac`), and `PureList`'s `List` the same (`Library/PureList.fss:41` at `a874948ac`); its compiled checker skipped a `where` clause on an `extends` entry ("TODO: Extend to handle non-empty where clauses.", `ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/scala_src/typechecker/TypeHierarchyChecker.scala:89`, `:125`, `:172` at `a874948ac`); the specification gave each `extends` entry an optional `where` clause in its grammar and no meaning (`Specification-1.0-frozen/basic/traits.tex:83`), and listed "where clauses and conditional extension" as not supported (`Specification-1.0-frozen/fortress/preamble.tex:82`). The tree now reads an `extends` entry whose `where` clause binds no variable and holds trait constraints only as extended exactly where the constraints hold, with the constraints holding inside the declaration, in the compiled checker: a pair is ordered where its elements are and a vector adds where its elements do (`ProjectFortress/compiler_tests/CondExtendChecker`), and a pair of functions is not ordered and a vector of strings does not add (`XXXCondExtendRefuseChecker`, exactly two errors). `LexicographicOrder` bounds its element by `StandardTotalOrder`, and `List` extends it under `E extends StandardTotalOrder[\E\]`, with `ZeroIndexed[\E\]` and `opr =(self, other: List[\E\])`, `LexicographicOrder`'s body, outside the condition (`Library/List.fsi:67-68`, `:83-85`; `Library/List.fss:106-107`, `:124-128`; `Library/PureList.fss:41-42`): the checker orders a list only where its elements are totally ordered, gives no order to a list of `RR64`, `QQ` or ranges, and compares a list of pairs element by element. Walk and the compiled run time still read the extension at every instance (`ProjectFortress/tests/XXXConditionalExtensionTypeTestWalk.fss`). The reason: the checker refused `LexicographicOrder`'s own comparison of two elements of an unbounded type, 'Could not check call to operator CMP ... is not applicable to an argument of type (E, E)' at `Library/FortressLibrary.fss:2063` (the distance stage's site, gone after the change); the text orders lists "whose elements support these same comparison operators" (`Specification/basic/operators/opr-overview.tex:294-295`), which no unconditional declaration can state; and without the element-wise `=` a list of pairs, not ordered, would fall to the identity `=` (`opr =(a:Any, b:Any)`), where `ProjectFortress/tests/NumberOrderListDeclarations.fss:48` compares such lists by `=`. The specification states the rule in `Specification/basic/trait-parameters.tex`, "Where Clauses", with its Appendix I entry "Conditional extension".
