# Record test: the key

For the grader. Two readers answered `questions.md`, each in its own copy of the repository: one copy holds the ten entries as they stand on `main` (the old texts), the other the new texts of `coordinator/process-engineering/record-rule-draft.md` Part 3, with the old texts moved to `FACTS-history.md` and `POSITIONS-history.md`. You score blind to the copy. For each scenario this key gives the right answer; each fact it rests on with where the fact lives (whether the old entry holds it, whether the new entry holds it, and the report, row or history that holds it otherwise); whether a reader of the new entry has to follow a pointer to answer well; what a wrong answer from missing information looks like; and the rubric.

Paths are under `explorations/` unless they start with `ProjectFortress/`, `Library/`, `Specification/`, `research/` or `.claude/`. "Row N" is a gap-ledger row, printed by `coordinator/tools/facts-extract.sh 'ledger:N'`. "The history file" is `coordinator/FACTS-history.md` or `coordinator/POSITIONS-history.md`, which in the new copy holds the old entry's text verbatim.

Scoring. **Decision 0-2**: 2, the decision is right and rests on the facts below; 1, right in the main but missing a fact that changes a step or a number; 0, wrong, or a step that cannot work, or a decision of the curator contradicted, or a re-measurement of what the record holds. **Sources 0-1**: 1, every load-bearing fact beyond the quoted entry is cited to a source that holds it (the sources named below, or an equivalent one), or the answer needed none; 0, such a fact is asserted without a source, invented, or the answer stops at the entry where the entry could not carry it.
