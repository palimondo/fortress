## Revival change

**The change.** The team's walk library declared `narrow` and `widen` on ZZ, bound to glue that keeps the low 32 and 64 bits (Library/FortressLibrary.fss:1150-1154 at 157323e51, the comment "This routine, named for consistency, converts to a ZZ64"), declared each of narrow, widen, signed, unsigned and big on some of the five integer types only, and declared no checked conversion on them; the team's later compiler library declares the checked getter asZZ32 of ZZ64 (`jLongOverflowingToInt`, ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fss:581) and the four bitsAs getters (:583, :668, :758, :824); the Working Draft of February 2011 names none of these conversions. The tree now gives each of ZZ32, ZZ64, NN32, NN64 and ZZ the getters asZZ32, asZZ64, asNN32, asNN64 and asZZ, which keep the value and raise IntegerOverflow where it does not fit; the fixed widths the bitsAs getters; narrow, widen, signed, unsigned and big on every type where each applies, keeping the bits; and ZZ no narrow or widen (tests/IntegerConversionsWalk.fss; under walk `narrow(z)` of a ZZ fails 'Failed to find any matching overload'). The numeral type IntLiteral's conversions, strings, = and cmp are Fortress over its asZZ (tests/IntLiteralTextWalk.fss). The reason, as evidence: under the team's walk library, `widen` of the ZZ 2^63 answered 'widen: -9223372036854775808' and `narrow` of the ZZ 2^32+5 'narrow: 5'; `widen` of the NN64 2^64-1 went through ZZ's `widen` by coercion and answered 'widen: -1 : ZZ64'; and `narrow` of the NN32 2^32-1 stopped with 'Ambiguous coercion, args = (4294967295: NN32)' (four probes run on the old code; on the tree they answer 'Failed to find any matching overload', the same, '18446744073709551615 : NN64' and 'narrow: 4294967295'). The team's later compiler library binds its plain asZZ32 to the native that raises (ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fss:581), and the specification says "For integer results, overflow throws an IntegerOverflow." (Specification/basic/operators/opr-overview.tex:155).
