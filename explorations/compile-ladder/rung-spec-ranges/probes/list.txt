The list of rung U (climb batch 7R, rung-spec-ranges), written before any edit, at 26c5d3dd7.

Every passage of Specification/ outside library/apis/, and every example of SpecData/examples/
the specification inputs, that names a range's integer type or builds a range over an integer
that is neither a ZZ32 nor a numeral; each with its file:line, what the decision makes of it,
whether it is revised now or left, and the source that settles it. In every file below except
basic-integers.tex and changes.tex (both revised by the revival since the import), the base's
lines equal the frozen copy's (diff, for ranges.tex, blocks.tex and defining-generators.tex).
"The decision": the ranges decision, POSITIONS.md 2026-09-27, the numerics plans, decision 1.
"J": the batch record's section for rung J, explorations/coordinator/CLIMB-BATCH-7R.md section 3.

How the list was made:
- measurement C's scanner, explorations/reviews/numerics-plan-coordinator/probes-C/nonzz32.py,
  re-run on this worktree (its ROOT changed, nothing else): the same 16 candidate lines as
  measurement C's nonzz32-candidates.txt, of which 7 are range constructions; the scanner
  reads the % lines of Specification/**/*.tex and the .fss files, and names typed ZZ64, NN32,
  NN64 or ZZ; it does not read typeset LaTeX, names typed NN (the specification's N), static
  nat parameters, or an untyped quantifier such as FORALL (m);
- by hand: every range-like line of SpecData/examples/**/*.fss (41 lines, each read); every
  line of Specification/ (outside library/) that holds a range glyph (COLONOP, char'43) or a
  Range type name with an integer type; every % or typeset block that pairs a wider integer
  type with a generator or range within six lines; every range bounded by a nat or int static
  parameter (a script over the % lines and the examples); the sentences "range over",
  "two different integer", "narrowest type" anywhere in Specification/ and in the later
  restart's .tick files.
