# Phase 3's exit: the zero, the decisions, the defaults

## 0. How to read this

- **The scale.** Each decision or default gets one of four verdicts. *Stands*: the designers' later word says this, or it finishes what they left in the direction they pointed, or it is a call in a gap they left and nothing since moves it. *Stands, text*: the decision is right, but its recorded text or reason is wrong and must be corrected. *Bends*: against a designers' text or design where they left a way the revival did not take; the ruling names what it bends and the designers' own way. *Reverse*: the decision should be withdrawn or turned.
- **Consequential** is the coordinator skill's test: it reverses, bends or reads anew a decision of his; touches microGPT's lines, speed or readability; chooses where the three principles split or are silent; or cannot be undone. A ruling so marked is his to take, and each carries a default he can take without reading the argument.
- **The three principles**, applied in order: the team's later word (P-26); the library as the team wrote it (P-27); what peer languages do, only where the first two are silent or split.
- **Costs** are in rungs. "Before" means before the switch-over's names batch, the batch in which the compiled path starts to read the one library and the compiler's prelude is deleted; "after" means after that batch. Phase 4's first batch, climb batch 23, moves native bindings only and reads nothing of what is ruled on here, so "before phase 4 starts" is a narrower point than "before the names batch".
- Abbreviations are the evidence map's: J0929, R0929, J1023 for the earlier judgements; rq N for a review-queue item; row N for a gap-ledger row; App I for `Specification/appendices/changes.tex`; WD for the Working Draft.
