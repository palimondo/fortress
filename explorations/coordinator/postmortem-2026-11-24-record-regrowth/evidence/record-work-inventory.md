## How to read the lines

A line reads: `HH:MM | what it was, tier | its label (the coordinator's own description) | what it was asked, one sentence from its brief | how many other agents ran at the time | what it produced | transcript file and line | mark`.

- **Transcript.** `A/002:19894` is part 002, line 19894, of the folder `fe616d40-a9c6-56d7-9da1-7168a172765d.until-2026-10-08T15-41.jsonl.parts/`. `B/001:3315` is the same in the folder `...until-2026-11-07T23-09.jsonl.parts/`. Both are under `/home/user/fortress-transcripts-blinded/projects/-home-user-fortress/`. The two folders overlap from 10-08 12:53 to 15:41; I count each launch once.
- **Tier.** The tier is the `model` of the Agent call, or the one in the agent's own file. "Fable coordinator", "Opus coordinator" and "Haiku coordinator" give the tier of the coordinating session itself, read from the `model` of its entries: Fable to 09-25 22:58; Opus to 10-02 10:00; Fable 10-02 10:00 to 10:13; Opus to 10-11 06:50; Fable to 10-12 21:19; Opus to 10-16 20:09; Haiku to 10-19 21:04; Opus after.
- **Alongside.** For an agent: the number of other agents of the whole session (background agents and the agents inside workflow runs, of any purpose) whose run overlapped the first 90 seconds after its launch. The agents' start and end are the first and last timestamps in their own files. For a workflow run: the most agents of the session at once during its span. For a hand edit: the same count at its first commit.
- **Produced.** "Commits in its report" are hashes that the agent's final message names and that git has. "Commits in its run window" are record commits whose time falls in the agent's run and that do not match a coordinator commit call: an attribution by time, which can be wrong when another agent committed meanwhile. "File" is the output file that the brief names.
- **By hand.** A commit is the coordinator's own when its author time is within 180 seconds after a `git commit` call in the coordinator's transcript. Commits of the coordinator that are less than 40 minutes apart stand on one line. Its Edit calls and its `python3` or `sed` scripts are not listed one by one; the half-hour count of Edit, Write and record-tool calls is only a hint of the work behind a commit.
- **Marks.** `[held: file section]` means that an evidence file of `explorations/coordinator/postmortem-2026-11-24-record-regrowth/evidence/` already describes this line. `[part: ...]` means that a file names it and not what it did. `[NOT HELD]` means that no file there holds it. Files: `consolidation-of-october.md`, `briefs-compared.md`, `sorting-by-topic.md`, `october-briefs/`, `his-words/`. I searched them for the line's label, hashes and time, and read the sections that bear on 10-13 and 10-22.
