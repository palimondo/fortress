## Revival change

**The change.** The team's compiled path printed an `NN32` or `NN64` at or above its signed half as a negative number: `FNN32`'s `toString` and `asString` were `String.valueOf` of the `int` (`git show a874948ac:ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/compiler/runtimeValues/FNN32.java`, `:19-20`), `FNN64`'s `toString` `"" + val` (`FNN64.java`, `:20`), and `simpleUnsignedLongArith.unsignedLongToString`, which its prelude's `NN64` `asString` calls, `Long.toString` (`simpleUnsignedLongArith.java`, `:23-25`, at `a874948ac`); so a compiled `n: NN32 = 4294967295` printed -1. Its `unsignedLongWrappingNeg` answered its operand (`:106-108`, at `a874948ac`), so its prelude's `NN64` prefix `BOXMINUS` was the identity. The tree prints the unsigned value (`ProjectFortress/compiler_tests/UnsignedPrintingCompiled`, `UnsignedLongToStringHelperCompiled`) and negates by wrapping (`compiler_tests/NN64WrappingNegationHelperCompiled`). The reason: an `NN32` or `NN64` is an integer of unsigned binary form (`Specification/basic-lib/basic-integers.tex:29-31`), so -1 names none; walk prints and negates so (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/interpreter/glue/prim/UnsignedLong.java:251-255`, `:365-369`); the team's own prelude masks its `NN32` getter (`ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fss:759`), and the wraparound subtraction of `Specification/basic/operators/opr-overview.tex`, "Addition and Subtraction Operators", gives the negation of x as 0 minus x, wrapped. The team's library declared `UnsignedLong`'s `asFloat` at walk's `Float` (`git show a874948ac:ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/FortressBuiltin.fss`, `:457`); the tree declares it at `RR64` (`ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/FortressBuiltin.fss:482`), under walk the same `Float` value (`tests/NativeHelperFamilyUnsignedWalk`), since the checker refuses the helper's `double` at `Float` ('Function body has type RR64, but declared return type is Float.') and the one library's `Number` declares `abstract asFloat(self): RR64` (`Library/FortressLibrary.fsi:290-291`; `Specification/basic-lib/numbers.tex:47-50`).
