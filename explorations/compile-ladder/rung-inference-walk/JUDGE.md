## 1. The refusal ground: the skeptic is right

- **The defect.** `coercionTypesByEnv` is a `private static final IdentityHashMap<Environment, List<FType>>` (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/interpreter/evaluator/values/Coercions.java:163-164`), filled by `coercionTypes` (`:170-187`) for the top level of the library, of the called function's component and of the value's type's component (`coercionTargets`, `:195-215`), and never cleared. - **The team's rule.** Every static cache of interpreter objects has a `reset()` that `Init.initializeEverything` calls (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/interpreter/evaluator/Init.java:26-44`: ten `reset()` calls beside four `resetState()` calls, among them `FTypeGeneric.reset()`, `ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/interpreter/evaluator/types/FTypeGeneric.java:52-54`; `GenericFunctionOrMethod.FunctionsAndState.reset()`, `evaluator/values/GenericFunctionOrMethod.java:70-72`; `NativeApp.reset()`, `interpreter/glue/NativeApp.java:221-224`, which also empties `NativeConstructor`'s list, `evaluator/values/NativeConstructor.java:34-39`), with the purpose written beside the reset's other caller, "to catch leaks in a seres of running tests" (`Init.java:51-52`). The rung's map is the one static cache of interpreter objects without one. Opened and checked: all as the skeptic cites them.
- **The repair, a decision taken here.** Three forms were offered (`SKEPTIC.md`, required correction 1):
  - (a) no cache: the reverse lookup would walk the library's top-level names for every argument of every lone-parameter inference; its cost is unmeasured, and the skeptic's timing with the cache found none (`probes/skeptic/warm-quickcheck.txt`), so dropping it risks the one thing measured as free;

  I take (c). Rule 2 of the batch asks for the team's shape where it exists, and here it is the only shape.
