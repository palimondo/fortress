## 2. What changed, and what settles each

The rules, their decisions and the landed code of both paths are tabled in `decision-record.md` section 2.

- **`narrow`, `LSHIFT` and `RSHIFT`** (`Specification/basic-lib/basic-integers.tex:39-66`). `narrow` of a `ZZ64`, `ZZ` or `NN64` keeps the low 32 bits. On a fixed width `n` and a count `k >= 0`, `x RSHIFT k` is `floor(x/2^k)` and `x LSHIFT k` is `x 2^k` reduced into the type modulo `2^n`. So a count at or beyond the width gives `0`, or `-1` for a negative value shifted right. A negative count shifts the other way. The count may be of any integer type and is read by its value. On `ZZ` the two operators are `shift(x,k)` and `shift(x,-k)`. Settled by POSITIONS 2026-09-22 (ledger rows 335, 346), 2026-09-24 (climb batch 3.5; ledger rows 380 and 381), and the natives: walk `Int.java:182-193`, `Long.java:194-206`, `NN32.java:188-200`, `UnsignedLong.java:189-201`, `Int.shiftCount` `Int.java:263-274`, `BigNum.java:228-254`, `:286-290`, `Long.java:250-254`, `UnsignedLong.java:245-249`; compiled `simpleIntArith.java:354-362`, `simpleLongArith.java:323-331`, `CompilerBuiltin.fss:647-648`, `:736-737`, `:659`, `:879`.
