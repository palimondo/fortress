## Revival change

**The change.** The Working Draft of February 2011 gives a numeral the types `NaturalNumeral[\n,10,v\]`, `RadixPointNumeral[\n,m,10,v\]`, their explicit-radix variants, `Literal[\v\]` and `Numeral[\n,m,r,v\]`, whose static arguments are the digit count, the digits after the radix point, the radix and the value, and says that "Numerals are not directly converted to any of the number types because, as in common mathematical usage, we expect them to be polymorphic" (`Specification-1.0-frozen/basic/expressions/literals.tex:83-141`); it says nothing of whether two string literals of one text are one object, and that a `nat` parameter "may ... appear in any context that a variable of type NN32 can appear" (`Specification-1.0-frozen/basic/trait-parameters.tex:83-86`). The revised specification says that every numeral is a `Numeral`, a `NaturalNumeral` or a `RadixPointNumeral` with no static parameters, its four numbers fields of its value; that a numeral converts where it stands, into the type its place fixes (a type with a coercion from the numeral type, or a numeral type), exactly into the integer types and `QQ` and with one rounding into `RR64` and `RR32`, out of range a static error, and elsewhere into `ZZ32`, `ZZ64` or `ZZ` by its value, or `RR64` with a radix point; that an expression of numerals alone folds exactly into a signed constant with those defaults and `QQ` for a quotient; that a program's type receives a numeral only through its own coercion, once per place; that a numeral type is written only as a parameter type; that a `nat` used as a value is a `ZZ32`; and that two string literals of one text are one object, a concatenation of literals folded into one (`Specification/basic/expressions/literals.tex:72-235`; `constant.tex:80-133`; `inference.tex:305-350`; `trait-parameters.tex:105-113`; `objects.tex:66-74`). No test: an edit of the specification has none; the specification's build checks it, and the rungs' tests show the behaviour on the merged tree. The reason, as evidence: the Working Draft gives numerals types of their own so that "library designers [can] decide how numerals should interact with other types of objects by defining coercion operations" (`Specification-1.0-frozen/basic/expressions/literals.tex:141-145`), which the team built under the names `IntLiteral` and `FloatLiteral` with the exact value at the coercion ("ZZ32 now coerces IntLiteral as described in the spec", commit `128f313b5b`, 2009) but never with the four numbers; a coercion handed a double rounds twice where one rounding from the exact value gives the nearest value; and every team source that speaks of literals makes equal literals one value ("Literals are values; they do not require evaluation", `literals.tex:25-26`; "All literals represent value objects", `Documentation/Specification/Prose/Language/Expressions/literals.tick:29`; `ProjectFortress/compiler_regressions/seqv.fss:22`).
