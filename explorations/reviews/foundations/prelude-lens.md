## 0. How to read this

- **The prelude.** The four wired files at the team's last commit `a874948ac`: `Library/CompilerLibrary.fsi/.fss`, `Library/CompilerAlgebra.fsi/.fss`, `Library/CompilerSystem.fsi/.fss`, `ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fsi/.fss`. Also `Library/GeneratorLibrary.fsi/.fss`, which no program reaches ("unwired"). A citation `CB.fsi:N` is `a874948ac:ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fsi:N`; `CL`, `CA` and `GL` are `CompilerLibrary`, `CompilerAlgebra` and `GeneratorLibrary` under `a874948ac:Library/`. A line cited "now" is in the current tree.
- **The one library.** `Library/` without the `Compiler*` files, and `ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/` without `CompilerBuiltin` (P-37).
- **Dating.** I dated a prelude line by its first appearance, as `explorations/reviews/foundations/bound-archaeology.md`, section 0, says: the earliest commit by commit time, among `git log --all --full-history -- PATH`, whose version of the file holds the line (a shell loop; section 8 gives it). Where that commit is a parentless snapshot, I say so: the snapshot is not where the line was written.
- **Grounds.** "L" marks a ground about the language. "J" marks a ground about the team's JVM implementation: the code generator, the run time, or the limits of the team's checker.
- **Weighed.** "Weighed" means that a record of the batch (its batch record, rung report, skeptic report, review or the curator's decision) names the prelude's way for this shape. "In substance" means that the record weighs the same alternative but does not name it as the prelude's. "Not weighed" means that I found no such record by grep (section 8).
- **The ask.** Point 1 to point 5 of the brief are the columns of section 2. Section 3 gives the changes where a deliberate prelude design differs and the batch did not weigh it. Section 4 gives the changes with no counterpart or only a stub. Section 5 is what I could not find.

## 1. In short

- **The overlap is small.** The prelude declares none of the types that most of the changes touch. In the four wired apis and in `GeneratorLibrary.fsi`, these names occur only in comments or not at all: `Index`, `ZeroIndexed`, `Indexed`, `ReadableArray`, `ImmutableArray`, `Array1`, `Vector`, `Integral`, `AnyIntegral`, `AdditiveGroup`, `MultiplicativeRing`, `StandardMin`, `StandardMax`, `StandardMinMax`, `StandardPartialOrder`, `Contains`, `HasRank`, `List`, `AnyList`, `NatParam`, `QQ`, `LexicographicOrder`, `ActualReduction`, `AssociativeReduction`, `RelationalPredicate*`, `ANDCOND`, `Maybe`, `AnyMaybe`, `Thread`, `distribute`, `builtinPrimitive`, `SumReduction`, `CatString`, `FlatString`, `OpenRange`, `Stream` (section 8, command 2). Only `GeneratorLibrary` has `BigReduction`, `Comprehension` and `MonoidReduction`.
- **Where the prelude has the shape, the batches mostly took its way or weighed it.** Taken: the flat numbers (P-43), `IntLiteral` (P-76), ranges over `ZZ32` (P-77), each integer type's own `MIN`, `MAX` and `MINMAX` (P-47), an integer power at its base's type (P-153), the component's restatement of an api's `abstract` member (P-158, which reversed batch 21b), `TotalComparison` below `Comparison` alone, `Comparison`'s lazy `LEXICO`, an unchecked `MatchFailure`, and `Range` excluding `String`. Weighed and not taken: the bottom-typed `fail`, the `comprises T` self type, `String`'s concatenation with `self` on the left only, `ZZ32Vector`'s two `fill`s, the integer-to-float coercions, and the `RR64` brackets.
- **Deliberate prelude designs that differ from what the revival did: three.** All three were weighed. Two were weighed without the team's dated commit that makes them the team's later word (section 3):
  1. `fail(s: String): Zilch`, a declared bottom result. Steele put it in place of `fail[\T extends Any\](s: String): T` on 2011-06-13 (`0886e9654`), with a parser production for it. The one library keeps the generic `fail`, and the checker's instance rule (P-48, `f3032eed8`) accepts it. No record cites `0886e9654`.
  2. `comprises T` as the self type of the order traits. Steele added `comprises T` to `StandardTotalOrder` on 2012-06-05 (`655e65d53`) and took its F-bound away the next day (`59fdeff62`). The one library keeps the F-bound, and the checker reads `self` from it (P-146, `1c32ccffd`; P-157, `64fa505c4`). The record weighs the idiom (way 5). It cites `59fdeff62` for its checker half only, never for this library edit.
  3. `String`'s `||`, `|||` and juxtaposition as functional methods with `self` on the left only and an `Object` right operand. The one library moved them to top-level operators (`0ae526b31`, `557cabb04`). Weighed by name (rung S of climb batch 21). The left-only restriction has no recorded reason.
- **Prelude shapes not weighed: three.** Each is a stub or of unclear status, so none is in section 3's list:
  - `Option` with one parent, `Condition`. The team's own commented drafts put `ZeroIndexed` under `Condition`.
  - `Generator`'s dotted `seq()`, with the functional `seq(self)` commented out.
  - `RR64`'s `^` at `RR64` and at `ZZ32`, a 2009 bootstrap.
- **Two side findings.** (a) The audit's list of excluded prelude commits (`zero-audit.md`, B.1) leaves out `108ab82d6` and `031c38bbc`, which also edit prelude files. The phase 3 judgement already covers both edits (`judgement.md`, 3.1, item 3). (b) The prelude's cast failure is unchecked (`object CastException extends UncheckedException`, `CL.fsi:83`). The one library's `CastError` is a `CheckedException` (`zero-audit.md`, A.4, row 352). This bears on the about 50 casts that the revival added, once row 352's `throws` check is built.
