### L5022 · 2026-11-08T23:11:00Z · coordinator
``````
You're not mixing things up. Natural numbers are the non-negative ones, and the Draft's numerals are exactly that: a numeral is digits, a radix point and a radix specifier, never a sign. `-3` is not a numeral; it is the prefix operator `-` applied to the numeral `3`. So `NaturalNumeral` is the right name for what the parser hands over, and the sign lives in the expression around it.

What that means under the design:
- At a typed place, `z: ZZ32 = -3` is `-(3)` with `3` converted first. The compiled path already folds this: a prefix minus on a numeral becomes a negative constant before the checker (F-262 records that a numeral's sign does not reach its type).
- With the fold of expressions of literals alone, `-3`, `3 + 4` and `1/2` become one exact signed value before anything else, and that value converts or defaults like a numeral. One trap this avoids: `-2147483648`, the smallest `ZZ32`. Read as `-(2147483648)` with the default first, the inner numeral doesn't fit `ZZ32`, becomes a `ZZ64`, and the result is a `ZZ64`; Java has a special grammar rule for this one literal. Folding first gives the exact value, which fits `ZZ32`.
- For a program's own type, `c: CC64 = -3` is `-(3)`: the coercion receives the numeral `3` with its four numbers, and the type's own prefix `-` applies to the result. A type that cannot be negated refuses it, as it should.

I put `-3` among the cases the judge checks, so its report says whether the design states this order, fold first, then convert or default, or leaves it to be said.
``````

``````
**Ask 4: should the compiled path run a reduction's halves in parallel, and when?**

Primer:
- A reduction is `SUM`, `DOT` or `BIG MAX` over a range or an array. The library splits the range into two halves, computes each, and joins the two results: `red.join(loop'(lo, mid-1), loop'(mid, hi))` (`RangeInternals.fss:1320`). Both halves are the arguments of one call to `join`.
- Under walk the arguments of a call run in parallel, so each half forks and the reduction is a tree of tasks. On the compiled path the code generator evaluates a call's arguments one after another, under the team's own comment "Why isn't this parallel???" (`CodeGen.java:6935-6955`).
``````
