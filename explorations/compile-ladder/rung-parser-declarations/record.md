## Revival change

**The change.**

- The team's parser reads U+2211 and U+220F as operator characters: `singleOp` takes any character that the operator table knows (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/parser/Symbol.rats:175-178` at `db352c1776`; `ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/parser_util/precedence_resolver/Operators.java:2080-2082`), and only the words `SUM` and `PROD` begin a reduction (`Symbol.rats:252-254` at `db352c1776`). So `∑[i <- 1#3] i` was a `Syntax Error`, and `∑ x` a call of a prefix operator named `SUM`. The tree now reads each sign exactly as its word: it begins a reduction and, after `opr`, declares `BIG +` or `BIG juxtaposition`; as an infix or postfix operator, or in an import's list, it is a `Syntax Error`. Tests: `ProjectFortress/tests/SumSignAccumulator.fss`, `ProjectFortress/compiler_tests/SumSignAccumulatorCompiled.test`. The reason: the specification's grammar, `Accumulator ::= ∑ | ∏ | BIG (Encloser | Op)` (`Specification/basic/expressions/reductions.tex:20`), and its operator header, which takes `∑` and `∏` (`Specification/appendices/grammars/concrete-syntax.tex:739-740`).
- The team's parser refuses, in an api, a postfix operator whose sign is an exponent operator, as `opr (x: ZZ32)^T: ZZ32`: `Syntax Error` at column 1 of the next line (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/parser/Parameter.rats:171-172` at `db352c1776`, which lacks the `/` of `:141-142`). The tree now takes in an api what it takes in a component. Tests: `ProjectFortress/tests/ApiPostfixExponentOp.fss`, `ProjectFortress/compiler_tests/ApiPostfixExponentOpCompiled.test`. The reason: the specification's grammar gives the api's header the same three alternatives as the component's (`Specification/appendices/grammars/concrete-syntax.tex:726-727`, `:737-738`).
