## Timeline

1. 18:22 to 18:25, the handover brief. Pavol asks for a summary he can restart from ("I worry you are... your contact is too large, and you are not at your optimal performance"). One verification precedes the writing — `MonoidReduction`, `ReductionWithZeroes`, `BigReduction` and `opr BIG` are confirmed present in `Library/*.fsi` — and `explorations/microgpt-native-brief.md` is committed with a Steele code extract as `5f1f92ca5`. The context is then compacted (`/compact` at 20:07) and the run proper opens at 20:10 with "Read explorations/microgpt-native-brief.md then execute it."

2. 20:11 to 20:28, the idiom probes, in the order the brief demands. `explorations/nprobe.fss` tests eight idioms from the 2015 talk against the 2012 interpreter: big-operator comprehensions with tuple generators, a user monoid registered through `CommutativeMonoidReduction` and `opr BIG OPLUS`, implicit tuple parallelism, `comprises` multimethods, `atomic`, `zip`, `split` — all work. P3, commandeering `SUM` itself, fails three ways: `Number comprises {RR64}` seals the bound, a competing nullary `opr SUM()` collides ("parameter lists have the same types" — the check ignores static parameters), and a generator-only overload is bypassed by the comprehension desugaring. `tparallel.fss` characterizes the interpreter's tuple parallelism and its performance law. Journal created; commit 3f374674b.

3. 20:29 to 20:49, three value-type alternatives at micrograd scale. `mgnative_a.fss` (uniform tape), `mgnative_b.fss` (sealed ADT with a `∂`-multimethod table), `mgnative_c.fss` (adjoint recursion with a memoized `opr DEL`). Every alternative reproduces every printed loss of the others bit for bit (final 5.5178095183714354E-11) at 17.5 s, 21.1 s and 24.4 s per 400-epoch loop. Syntax costs along the way: a trailing `||` at end of line, `a.data^n` needing parentheses, and the `FloatLiteral` inference that makes `[\RR64\]` ascriptions on list literals load-bearing. Commits 6cfdfd723 and, after the Fortify render pass renamed `xa`/`xb` to `x1`/`x2`, 2f74703c4.

## Findings

The brief ordered the probes before any design and the run obeyed it: `nprobe.fss` was written and running within three minutes of the restart, and its P3 result — that the library's `SUM` cannot be commandeered — fixed the notation of the next two days, since every summation in both programs then wore a custom big operator instead.

The authority the run consulted was the interpreter and the library, never the specification. The process audit puts numbers on it: across the design phase, 106 interpreter runs and 29 library reads against zero queries into `Specification/`, and the 161 earlier spec-path touches in the session were PDF building and typography. Two of the three impossibility claims the run recorded had sanctioned escapes sitting in the spec — the Meet Rule for the vector×matrix overload, and the `BIG` operator's generator form for `SUM` — and both claims survived into the shipped program as ⊞ and ⊕.

The exploration of alternatives happened at micrograd scale and not at transformer scale. Three engines were built, measured and shown bit-identical on the same trajectory, which is the brief's rule applied; at the transformer level each rung had exactly one design, which is what Pavol's 2026-08-25 review named and what forced the second implementation four hours later.

Everything the run claimed to run, ran, and every code claim had a machine gate: `microgpt_native.fss` and `microgpt_paper.fss` each passed v1's golden check at 1e-9 on the first attempt with the same 1264 parameters, and the coordinator re-ran the gate itself in the main checkout before each commit rather than trusting the worker's report. The errors the round had to retract were of the other kind — negative claims and forecasts made without a gate: the compiled path's "constructs still `sayWhat`" folklore, the port doc's "Python's Mersenne Twister is not ours", ⊞'s claimed literature precedent.

Prior work in the tree was used as material: v1's hardcoded Python-reference values as the anchor, the `mgbench` timing pattern for the measurements, `explorations/complex_ring.fss` as the precedent the spec audit was told to hold the autodiff type against, and the in-tree `doubledOverloading3.fss` as corroboration that the overload limitation was doubted in 2012 as well.

Time went into three things that did not survive: the first two worker launches, killed by interrupts minutes after they started and relaunched from scratch; the four-solutions presentation of 2026-08-25, thrown away eighteen hours later; and the ⊕/⊞ notation, which was carried from the 2015 slides on day one and retired at the end of the round. The delegation pattern changed inside the run on two standing orders, from long self-written briefs to pointer briefs a third the length, and the later worker briefs are visibly shorter for it.

The effort divides as 2.6e5 output tokens and 7.7e7 context in the coordinator against about 1.2e6 tokens across six workers, for two verified programs of 378 and 431 lines, a 12-entry compiler-gap catalog with 34 probes, a 20-finding spec audit with 26 probe files, a PRNG study with a chart, and one published page that was rebuilt twice inside the window.
