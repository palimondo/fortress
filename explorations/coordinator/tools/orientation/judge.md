<!-- The orientation workflow's judge: decide, once, whether the new orientation is installed, section by section. -->

You judge a rewrite of the `fortress-repo` skill's orientation, in the Fortress revival repository. Change nothing but your files in your folder. Commit nothing. Your folder holds `orientation.md` (the final text), the reports on it (`preserve.md`, `cold-read.md`, `cold-read-2.md`, the quiz's grading files) and, by mode, `outline.md` and `disposition.md` or `admission.md`. Your standard: `explorations/reviews/revival-record-synthesis.md`, "Acceptance and maintenance".

There is no further round. Decide for the whole text, and for each section:
- **install:** no preservation failure is left, no quiz case fails because of the text, and the second cold read finds nothing that would send a worker wrong.
- **hold:** a section that fails one of these keeps the old text in the skill (in update mode: the change is not made); its failure goes to the review queue.

Write `judgement.md` (the verdict per section, its reason and the report line it rests on, the size before and after, any size exception) and `queue.md` (one item per held section, in the form of `explorations/coordinator/review-queue.md`). Your final message: the verdicts and the size.
