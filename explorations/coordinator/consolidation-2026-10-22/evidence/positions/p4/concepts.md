# Concepts for every worker, chunk p4

An entry defines a concept only when that entry alone uses it, or when it is the decision that makes the concept: a left zero (P-57), a native binding (P-58), a ground declaration (P-60), the value reading of `comprises` and "covers" (P-60, which decides them). The concepts below are used by several entries, so they belong in the skill, and the entries name them without explaining them. The skill's core already defines walk, the compiled path, the switch-over, sizes as static parameters, `comprises` and the class loader; `references/tests-writing.md` defines expected failures, `references/specification.md` the revision form and `references/interpreter.md` and `references/compiler.md` the glue classes and `nativeHelpers/`.

| Term | Entries | Where to define it | What the definition needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| Batch and rung | P-54, P-58, P-62 ("a rung after the switch-over", "the first natives rung", "owed in a batch rung") | `fortress-repo` core, or the short orientation | A batch is one run of the batch workflow that changes the tree in several rungs; a rung is one task of it, with its own worker and worktree (the `coordinator` skill's glossary). An entry's "a rung tries" or "a rung after the switch-over" is work that a future batch takes. No part of `fortress-repo` defines either word. |
| The checker count | P-57; F-53 owns it | `fortress-repo`, `references/gate.md` or `references/compiler.md` | The errors that the compiled checker reports on the interpreter's library, up to each component's first error, counted by a stage of the gate, by component and api; its zero is the aim before the switch-over. Beside it, the distance: all the checker's errors on that library. The `coordinator` skill's `references/batch.md` defines both; workers do not load it. |
| Cover, and the closed trait | P-60, P-147; also P-44 and P-149 | `fortress-repo` core, "Fortress as a language", the bullet on traits | A type covers another when every value of the second is a value of the first. A trait with a `comprises` clause is closed, and covered by its listed types. Covering is not subtyping: another trait may stand between a closed trait and its listed types. P-60 decides this reading; the core's dispatch bullet already says "covers their meet" without defining it. |
| The Meet Rule | P-60 (with "a call's tie", several most specific declarations); the pilot's P-45 | `fortress-repo` core, the dispatch bullet | The overloading rule that two overloads whose parameter types overlap, neither more specific, need a declaration at their meet, or, by P-60, declarations below both that together cover it. The core states the rule without its name, and the record and the specification use the name. |
| PLAN's phases | P-54, P-58, P-59 (phase 4); chunk p3 lists the concept with P-46 and P-48 | `fortress-repo` core, the list in "The record" | Phase 4 is the switch-over, and it holds the natives rungs and P-68's rung on rounding. See chunk p3's row. |
| The revision form, which the record calls the S1 form | P-147 (and P-54's revised paragraph); P-96 decides it | the short orientation, or one sentence in `references/specification.md` | `references/specification.md`, "Changing the text: the revision form", defines it. The record names the same form "the S1 form" (P-96). One name is needed: an entry that says "S1" cannot be read from the skill. |
| Welterweight | P-147; P-60's addendum | `references/exploring.md`, the list of the team's sources | The type group's 2012 calculus (`Papers/Welterweight/`), with its Covered rule for abstract declarations. Chunk p3's row "The team's 2012 texts on `comprises`" holds the same need. |
| `QQ`'s infinities and 0/0 | P-54; review-queue item 25 | `references/library.md`, beside the number types | `QQ`, the rationals, holds +∞ (1/0), −∞ (−1/0) and the undefined 0/0, as the team built it (the coordinator's text of 2026-10-09T07:19:47.208Z). The skill names `QQ` among the number types but not these three values, and P-54's rounding rules are about them. |
| The comparison types | P-57; also the orders and `LEXICO` | `references/library.md` | `Comparison` comprises `Unordered` and the trait `TotalComparison`, which comprises `LessThan`, `EqualTo` and `GreaterThan` (`Library/FortressLibrary.fss:132-206`). `Unordered` is the outcome of `a CMP b` when `a` and `b` are partially ordered and no ordering relationship exists between them (`:149-150`). P-57 gives the one clause that it needs; the full list belongs to the library part. |
| The sign-refined number types | P-62; P-80 decides them | `references/library.md`, beside the flat number types | The specification's original design of eighteen rational types, instantiations of one trait `RationalQuantity` with flags, kept word for word as a superseded design (`Specification/advanced-lib/numbers-advanced.tex`). A program tests such a subset at run time instead. |
