8.1 What is compared with what

A second program, MicroGptFlatQuick.fss, checks that the model computes the right numbers. It runs the model and compares its results with values that another program computed.

The other program is written in Python with NumPy. Its file, microgpt_flat.py, is not in the repository. It has the same data layout, and it starts from the same parameter files. A script, make_goldens.py, ran it once and stored its results as text files, one number on each line, in the folder explorations/run-c/goldens. The record calls these stored values goldens.

A run of the quick check under walk printed this. The times are left out, as in Part 7.

VERDICT: 7 PASS, 0 FAIL of 7 -- ALL PASS

The seven checks:

  1. The loader. The 4,192 parameters that loadParamsloadParams reads are equal to the stored vector, to the last digit.

  2. The corpus. The first 16 rows of the token matrix, and the 16 lengths, are equal to the stored rows.

  3. The loss of the step on document 0, yuheng: 3.365966947584851 in both programs.

  4. The gradient of that step. The largest difference over the 4,192 entries is 0.00000000000000011.

  5. The number of gradient entries that are exactly 0: 464, the count of Part 6.

  6. The parameters after one Adam step. The largest difference is 0.00000000000000028.

  7. The loss of the step on document 1 with the new parameters: 3.42427278387177 in both, with a difference in the sixteenth digit.

The differences in checks 4, 6 and 7 are near 0.0000000000000001, the last digit that an ℝ64RR64 keeps. The check's tolerance is 0.000000000001, ten thousand times larger.

Checks 3, 5 and 7 are numbers of earlier parts: the loss of Part 5, the zeros of Part 6, and the second loss of the training loop in Part 7.

  • Lines 18 to 20 are constants with written types. The two tolerances are 10 to the power -12.

  • Lines 22 and 23 declare two top-level variables that can change: the form is name: Type := valuename: Type := value, as in the training loop.

  • reportreport prints one line of the output above and counts it. diff ≤ toldiff <= toldiff ≤ tol is a comparison, and okok is a BooleanBoolean. passed += 1passed += 1 adds 1 to the variable.

  • maxAbsDiffmaxAbsDiff is the largest difference between two vectors. MAXBIG MAX is used here with a generator: ii runs over the indices, and the expression after the brackets is evaluated for each ii. Around a number, the two bars give the absolute value: |ai − bi||a[i] - b[i]| is the difference without its sign. Around an array they give the size, as in |a||a|. Fortress chooses the meaning by the type of what stands between the bars.

8.3 Running it, and the full check

From the folder of the program:

cd explorations/run-c4/src
../../../bin/fortress MicroGptFlatQuick.fss

The project's script explorations/coordinator/tools/mg-run.sh does the same from an empty cache and keeps the output in a file. By your ruling, the check of microGPT after a batch of work on the tree must take less than a minute. This program is that check.

A longer program beside it, MicroGptFlatCheck.fss, makes 40 checks. It runs five Adam steps, a batch of four documents, and the experiment of Part 6 on eleven parameters: it changes one parameter, calls the model twice, and compares the measured rate with the gradient's entry.
