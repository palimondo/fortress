# Record lines for rung N (`rung-integral-ops`)

## FACTS.md

Three lines, for the ladder / compiler-prelude section.

- **The compiler prelude has `MOD`, `REM`, `GCD`, `LCM`, `LSHIFT` and `RSHIFT` on `ZZ32` and `ZZ64`, in pure Fortress and with no native helper** (rung N, 2026-09-19, `explorations/compile-ladder/rung-integral-ops/REPORT.md`): twelve declarations at `ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fsi:176-179`, `:196-197` (`ZZ64`) and `:237-240`, `:257-258` (`ZZ32`), and twelve bodies at `CompilerBuiltin.fss:584-606`, `:630-631` (`ZZ64`) and `:667-689`, `:717-718` (`ZZ32`). `REM` is the specification's own identity `m REM n = m - n(m ÷ n)` (`Specification/basic-lib/basic-integers.tex:484`) over the trait's `DIV`, `-` and juxtaposition, so it inherits `DIV`'s `DivisionByZero`, with one guard: the divisor `-1` returns `0` directly, because `x REM -1` is `0` for every `x` while `MIN DIV -1` overflows legitimately on a fixed-size type and the gated corpus pins that it must (`ProjectFortress/library_tests/Integer3.fss:127`, `Integer4.fss:127`). `MOD` is `REM` with the interpreter's four-branch sign correction (`glue/prim/Int.java:307-318`); `GCD` is Euclid over `REM` in the `var`/`while` shape `trait ZZ`'s own `CHOOSE` already uses (`CompilerBuiltin.fss:538-552`), normalized to nonnegative at the end; `LCM` is over `GCD` and `DIV`; `LSHIFT` and `RSHIFT` are names for the `<<` and `>>` of the same trait. `MOD` and `GCD` reach the `-1` guard through `REM`, so one guard covers all three. Gated by `ProjectFortress/library_tests/IntegralOpsRungN.{fss,test}`, which pins all four sign combinations of `REM` and `MOD` against the specification's table (`basic-integers.tex:465-468`), the `GCD`/`LCM` prose (`:523-529`), the lattice property (`:544`), the shifts against `<<`/`>>`, division by zero, the divisor `-1` and the type minimum for `REM`, `MOD` and `GCD` on both `ZZ32` and `ZZ64`, the two boundary cases whose true result is `2^31` and which therefore must throw, and twenty-two `ZZ64` cases beyond `ZZ32`. `NN32` and `NN64` were deliberately left out.
- **A binary operator applied to two integer literals resolves by coercion, and the expected type at the call site picks the trait — unless `trait IntLiteral` declares that operator itself, in which case its own overload wins** (rung N, 2026-09-19, probes at `explorations/compile-ladder/rung-integral-ops/probes/`): `3 << 4` answers `48` although `<<` is declared four times over on each of four numeric traits (`probes/LitResolve2.fss`), `t(3 << 4, 48)` with `t(x:ZZ32, y:ZZ32)` and `u(3 << 4, 48)` with `u(x:ZZ64, y:ZZ64)` both pass (`probes/LitTarget.fss`), and `3 BITAND 4` throws `CompilerFailureDetectedAtRunTime` from rung 7's stub at `CompilerBuiltin.fss:895` (`probes/LitResolve2.fss`). The folding phase is not involved: `desugarer/IntegerLiteralFoldingVisitor.java:105-106` returns the node untouched for every operator outside its list, and `MOD`, `REM`, `GCD`, `LCM`, `<<` and `>>` are all outside it. The consequence for prelude work: a name that literal-literal call sites use must **not** be declared on `IntLiteral` unless it can be given a real body there. The interpreter world reached the same conclusion first and left the evidence in place: all six of these operators are written out on `object IntLiteral` at `ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/FortressBuiltin.fss:501-508` and `:517-520` (api `FortressBuiltin.fsi:130-133`, `:138-139`) inside a comment that opens at `.fss:483` and reads "Do not enable these until coercion is implemented; doing so will cause all our arithmetic to occur on IntLiterals" (`:484-485`).
- **`DivisionByZero` can be caught on the compiled path**, so the five `shouldDivideByZero` assertions that `library_tests/Integer3.fss:87-91` leaves commented out — recorded as dormant with status "unknown" at `explorations/coordinator/map/dormant-code.md:314` — would pass if uncommented (rung N, 2026-09-19, `explorations/compile-ladder/rung-integral-ops/probes/CatchDivZero.fss`, output `CAUGHT DivisionByZero`). The scope is exact and matters: the compiled path is the only path `library_tests/Integer.test` drives, since its body is `compile`, `link`, `run` (`ProjectFortress/library_tests/Integer.test:11-13`, with `tests=` at `:10` and `run_out_WIcontains=PASS` at `:14`), so the status at `dormant-code.md:314` resolves to "works on the compiled path" rather than simply "works". Under `walk` the same division is not a Fortress exception at all but an uncaught `java.lang.ArithmeticException: / by zero` from `Int$Div.f`, a bare `x / y` (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/interpreter/glue/prim/Int.java:118-122`), which a Fortress `try`/`catch` does not see — provisional ledger row 332 below. Why the team commented those five lines out remains unknown and rung N does not guess. Rung N pins the same division-by-zero behaviour for `REM` and `MOD` in its own test rather than editing a gated test file; uncommenting those five lines is unclaimed.
