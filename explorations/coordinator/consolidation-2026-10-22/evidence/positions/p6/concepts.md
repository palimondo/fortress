# Concepts for every worker, chunk p6

The entries define a concept only when that entry alone uses it. Each concept below is used by several entries, or by the skill's parts, so the skill should define it, and the entries name it without explaining it.

| Term | Entries | Where to define it | What the definition needs |
|---|---|---|---|
| The integer types by width: fixed-width and unbounded | P-69, P-70, P-71, P-72, P-73, P-75 | `fortress-repo` core, the bullet "Numbers are siblings" | `ZZ32` and `ZZ64` are the signed 32-bit and 64-bit integers, `NN32` and `NN64` the unsigned ones: the four fixed-width types. `ZZ` is the unbounded integers, ℤ, and cannot overflow. The core lists the eight number types but says neither which are fixed-width nor that `ZZ` is unbounded. |
| `IntLiteral`, the type of an integer numeral | P-76, P-74, P-75 | `references/library.md`, beside the conversions into `RR64`; one clause in the core's numbers bullet | The checker types every integer numeral as `IntLiteral`, a sibling under `Number`, which the five integer types, `QQ` and `RR64` convert from. Walk gives a numeral the type `ZZ32`, or `ZZ64` or `ZZ` when its value does not fit, until walk has the checker's static types. `tests-writing.md` and `library.md` name numerals, and only `revival-changes.md` names `IntLiteral`. |
| The wrapping and saturating operators | P-73, P-66, P-38 | `references/library.md`, the bullet on wrapping | `library.md` defines the wrapping operators ∔, ∸ and ⨰. It should add the saturating ones, ⊞ (`BOXPLUS`), ⊟ (`BOXMINUS`), and ⊡ or ⊠ for multiplication: the specification states them, the interpreter's library does not declare them, and the compiler's prelude swaps the two families' spellings until the switch-over (ledger row 348), so a compiled test that spells ∔ `DOTPLUS` gets saturation. |
| A big operator written with generators, and its static argument | P-78, P-79 | `references/library.md`, the bullet on `SUM` and `PROD` | The two forms: `SUM[\ZZ32\][j <- 0#n] f(j)`, with generators, and `SUM e`, over a collection `e`. Only the first can lack the element type, and only it writes a static argument. `library.md` gives the rule for the first form and does not name the second. |
| PLAN and its lists | P-75, P-76, and most entries with a "Still to build" line | The short orientation that replaces the revival part | `explorations/coordinator/PLAN.md` holds the phases of the work (phase 4 is the switch-over, phase 5 microGPT compiling), each with its lines, and the list "Pavol's answers, in the order they are needed", whose numbered items wait for the curator.
