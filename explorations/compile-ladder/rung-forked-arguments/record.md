## Revival change

**The change.**

- *A call's arguments on the compiled path.* The team's code generator evaluates a tuple argument's elements in order, under its comment "Why isn't this parallel???" (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/compiler/codegen/CodeGen.java`, `evalArg`, at the team's last commit a874948ac), while the specification gives each argument an implicit thread of its own (`Specification/basic/evaluation/parallelism.tex:60-64`) and walk forks them (`ProjectFortress/src/com/sun/fortress/interpreter/evaluator/Evaluator.java:342`). The tree now forks the arguments of a function call and of a dotted method call where the parallelism analyzer marked the argument tuple: two or more arguments that are calls of the program's named functions. So such arguments may run at once, their side effects in any order, and a compiled program must not rely on left-to-right arguments. Test: `compiler_tests/CallArgumentsForkCompiled`. Evidence: 'a function's two arguments: 1,2, at most 1 at once' before, 'at most 2 at once' after. The calls written in the compiler's prelude do not fork, so its `SUM` still runs one body at a time.
- *A group that throws, on the compiled path.* The team's code generator joins the forked arms of a `do … also`, a tuple or an operator on the normal path only (`CodeGen.java`, `genParallelExprs`, at a874948ac), so an arm ran on after its group had thrown; the specification has a group complete only when each of its threads has (`parallelism.tex:82-100`), and walk joins every arm (`Evaluator.java:275`). The tree now waits for every arm that was forked and then throws the exception of the first arm, in the arms' order, that threw; an arm that was never forked is not run. Test: `compiler_tests/AlsoArmThrowsJoinsCompiled`. Evidence: 'after the catch: flag 0, later: flag 1' before, 'flag 1, later: flag 1' after.
- *A task arm.* In the team's code generator a local, a `try` or an `atomic` block in an arm after the first gives a class that fails the JVM's verification (`CodeGen.java`, `generateTaskCompute`, at a874948ac). The tree compiles them. Tests: `compiler_tests/TaskArmLocalSlot`, `TryInArmRungG`, `AtomicEndsArmCompiled`. An `atomic` block or a `try` as a call's later argument that does not fork still fails so (`compiler_tests/XXXAtomicBlockLaterArgument`): bind its value in a statement of its own.
- *The parallel range under walk.* The team's library splits a parallel `ZZ32` range down to single elements, each a task (`Library/RangeInternals.fss`, `CompactFullParScalarRange`, at a874948ac). The tree stops the split at 16 elements and runs each chunk by the sequential range's methods (`Library/RangeInternals.fss:20`, `:1320-1321`, `:1337-1338`). So under walk a `for`, a comprehension or a reduction over a `lo#n` or `lo:hi` range of sixteen elements or fewer runs its bodies one after another, in index order, and a longer one in chunks of at most 16; a test of parallelism needs a range longer than 16, and a floating-point sum can differ in its last bits from the team's, since the chunks are summed in order. A range with a stride still splits to single elements. Test: `tests/ParRangeGrainWalk`. Evidence: the bodies of `0#16` ran '0 4 8 6 1 5 9 7 10 12 3 2 11 13 14 15' before and in index order after; the specification lets an implementation serialize any part of a group (`parallelism.tex:88-90`).
