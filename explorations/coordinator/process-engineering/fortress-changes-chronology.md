# What the revival changed in Fortress, and whose Fortress the skill describes

## How to read this

- Short lists. Numbers as K or M. A term is defined where it first appears.
- **The team**: the Sun Labs designers. **The team's tree**: this repository at `a874948ac`, their last commit (2012-08-31). **The revival**: every commit after it.
- **Walk**: the interpreter, `bin/fortress P.fss`. **The compiled path**: `fortress compile` then `fortress run`; it makes JVM bytecode. **The checker**: the static type checker, written in Scala; it runs on the compiled path only.
- **The library**: the Fortress files in `Library/` and `ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/`. There are two. The interpreter's, `FortressLibrary` and `FortressBuiltin`, is called **the one library** here, because the curator chose it to become the only one. The compiler's own, **the prelude** (`CompilerLibrary`, `CompilerBuiltin`, `CompilerAlgebra`), is deleted at the **switch-over**, the day the checker accepts the one library (POSITIONS, "The library route."; "The compiler prelude's leftovers go at the switch-over.").
- **Batch and rung**: a batch is one run of the climb workflow; a rung is one worker's piece of it. Batches are named 1 to 10 and 3.5, 6b, 7R, 7C, 6.5, 6.5b, N and 7b, after "the ladder climb" (eight rungs, 09-17) and "the repair batch" (09-18). Each batch's record is `explorations/compile-ladder/climb-batch-*/RECORD.md`; batches 1 and 2 have `explorations/coordinator/CLIMB-BATCH-1.md` and `-2.md`. Which commits each batch landed is in `record-growth/landings.csv` (beside this note) and in section A10.
- **Row**: a line of the gap ledger, `explorations/fortress-gap-ledger.md`, cited by its number.
- **POSITIONS** holds the curator's decisions, **FACTS** what is established, both in `explorations/coordinator/`. A cited title is the entry's bold opening. **Answer N** is one of the curator's numbered answers of 2026-09-26 to 09-29 (answers 7 to 10 here).
- **Callout, S1 form, Appendix I**: how the revival changes the specification. A printed callout sits at the changed passage, and an entry in Appendix I (`Specification/appendices/changes.tex`) quotes the original and gives the reason (POSITIONS, "The S1 form"). `Specification-1.0-frozen/` is the unrevised specification: the Working Draft of 2011-02-02, byte for byte as the team left it.
- **Marks**. **[V]**: a programmer writing Fortress would notice it: a different answer, a program refused or accepted, or a different library declaration. **[I]**: internal.
- The tag `sealed-tree` is `75cca6683` (2026-09-16), the last commit before the revival edited the original sources. Its message: each later edit starts from a failing test added to the corpus (POSITIONS, "Test first, the test kept.").
- Evidence from the team's tree is cited as `a874948ac:path:line`, read with `git show a874948ac:path`.

The suites: about 1.4K tests in `testFast` and 382 in `testSystem` on the team's tree, first run with the build moved to JDK 8 (`explorations/test-baseline-jdk8.md`); 1.8K and 516 at batch 10's gate (`compile-ladder/climb-batch-10/gate/summary.txt`).

### A2. The library's numbers

- **N1 [V] The number types are siblings under `Number`, not a tower.**
  - The team left: `ZZ32` extends `ZZ64`, which extends `ZZ`; `NN32` extends `NN64`, which extends `ZZ`; `ZZ` reaches `AnyIntegral`, then `QQ`, then `RR64`; `RR32` extends `RR64`; `IntLiteral` extends `ZZ32` (`a874948ac:Library/FortressLibrary.fsi:335,370,406,434,461,495,533`; `ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/FortressBuiltin.fsi:47,82,113`). The team's compiler prelude was already flat, each type excluding the others (`LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fsi:102-443`).
  - Now: `ZZ32`, `ZZ64`, `NN32`, `NN64`, `ZZ`, `QQ`, `RR64` and `RR32` are siblings (`Number comprises { RR64, RR32, QQ, AnyIntegral }`, `AnyIntegral comprises { ZZ, ZZ64, ZZ32, NN64, NN32 }`), each with its own algebra; each wider type declares `coerce` from each narrower one. `RR64` converts from `ZZ32` and `RR32` only.
  - Commits: `d846e3644` (batch 6, rung F); `e455ccd98` for `RR32` (N11).
  - Decision: POSITIONS, "The exclusion rule stays and the tower is flat (route A)." and "Mixed widths convert by declared coercions (answer 8)."; FACTS, "The one library's number tower is flat"; `compile-ladder/climb-batch-6/RECORD.md`; PLAN, phase 2.
- **N2 [V] `SUM` and `PROD` are generic, and a sum's identity comes from the static argument.**
  - The team left: `opr SUM[\T extends Number\](g: Generator[\T\]): Number`, and the big operators `BIG MAXN`, `BIG MINN` and `BIG MINMAXN` over `Number` (`a874948ac:Library/FortressLibrary.fsi:1820-1822,1842-1851`).
  - Now: one generic `SUM` and one `PROD` over the library's `AdditiveGroup` and `MultiplicativeRing`, the identity chosen by a `() -> T` witness `typecase`; the three `...N` operators are gone. A clause form whose element type nothing fixes writes its static argument: `SUM[\ZZ32\][j <- 0#i] f(j)`. Two lines of the model program changed with it (`MicroGptFlat.fss:43` and `:28`, approved as diffs).
  - Commit: `d846e3644`.
  - Decision: POSITIONS, "`SUM` and `PROD` without the `Number` catch-all (answer 7)." and "The team demos that write no static argument for a generic reduction ..."; FACTS, "The one library's number tower is flat"; PLAN, phase 2.
- **N3 [V] `QQ` is exact.** The team left `QQ`'s `<` cross-multiplying its parts in whatever width they held, so fixed-width parts wrapped and `(widen(1) LSHIFT 62) < 1/4` was `true` (row 428). - **N4 [V] Fixed-width arithmetic raises `IntegerOverflow` under walk.**

  - Now: walk's ten signed and eight unsigned natives raise the catchable `IntegerOverflow`; `^`, `CHOOSE` and `LCM` too, and `CHOOSE` answers 0 for `k < 0` or `k > n`.
  - Commits: `917bb7b32` (batch 6b, rung O), `413f36ac0` (batch 6.5b, rung E, the powers).
  - Decision: POSITIONS, "Fixed-width overflow raises `IntegerOverflow` under `walk`"; FACTS, "Under `walk`, fixed-width integer arithmetic raises `IntegerOverflow` when the result does not fit, as the specification says"; `climb-batch-6b/RECORD.md`.
- **N5 [V] The specification's wrapping operators exist in the library.** The team's specification names `∔`, `∸` and `⨰` (wraparound `+`, `-` and `*`); no running library declared them (they were in a 2007 draft).
