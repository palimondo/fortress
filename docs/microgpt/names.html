10.1 The names of the forward pass

The sizes are those that a run printed for a batch of one document. "16 by 16" means 16 rows by 16 columns. A size with three numbers means planes, rows, columns.

  • pp: the parameters. A vector of 4,192 floats.

  • bb: the batch, the numbers of the documents of this step. Here one integer, 0.

  • idsids: the tokens' identifiers. The token that the model reads in each row. 16 integers.

  • tgtg: the targets. The token that comes next in each row, the right answer. 16 integers.

  • vmvm: the mask of the valid rows. 1.0 for a row that counts, 0.0 for a row after the name's end. 16 floats.

  • nvnv: the number of valid rows, the rows that count. 7.0 for yuheng.

  • pospos: the positions. The position of each row, 0 to 15. 16 integers.

  • wtewte: the weights of the token embedding. The token table, 27 by 16. Row tt is the embedding of the token tt. A view into pp.

  • wpewpe: the weights of the position embedding. The position table, 16 by 16. Row ii is the embedding of position ii. A view into pp.

  • lmlm: the language model's head, in the files lm_head. The matrix that makes 27 scores from a row, 27 by 16. A view into pp.

  • wqwq, wkwk, wvwv: attention's weights for the query, the key and the value, in the files attn_wq, attn_wk and attn_wv. The matrices that make queries, keys and values, 16 by 16 each. Views into pp.

  • wowo: attention's weights for the output, in the files attn_wo. The matrix that mixes the heads' results, 16 by 16. A view into pp.

  • f1f1: in the files mlp_fc1, for multi-layer perceptron, fully connected, 1. The first matrix of the feed-forward block, 64 by 16. f2f2: the second, 16 by 64. Views into pp.

  • xx: the token's embedding plus the position's embedding, for each row. 16 by 16.

  • xpxp: xx with rmsnrmsn, the RMS norm, on every row. 16 by 16. Attention's result is added to it.

  • x1x1: xpxp with rmsnrmsn on every row. 16 by 16. Attention reads it.

  • qq, kk, vv: the queries, the keys and the values. Every row of x1x1 through wqwq, wkwk, wvwv: the query, the key and the value of each position. 16 by 16 each.

  • qhqh, khkh, vhvh: the queries, keys and values in heads. The same numbers cut into heads. 4 planes of 16 by 4 each. Views.

  • aa: attention. The attention weights. 4 planes of 16 by 16. In a plane, row ii holds the attention weights with which position ii reads positions 0 to 15. Each row adds up to 1.

  • hchc: the heads' results. For each position, the four heads' weighted averages of the values, side by side. 16 by 16.

  • x2x2: xpxp plus hchc through wowo. 16 by 16. The feed-forward block's result is added to it.

  • x3x3: x2x2 with rmsnrmsn on every row. 16 by 16.

  • m0m0: every row of x3x3 through f1f1. 16 by 64.

  • mrmr: m0m0 after ReLU, with its negative numbers set to 0. 16 by 64.

  • x4x4: x2x2 plus mrmr through f2f2. 16 by 16.

  • prpr: the probabilities, the model's answer. 16 by 27. Row rr holds the 27 probabilities for the token after position rr.

  • lossloss: one float. The mean, over the rows that count, of minus the logarithm of the probability of the right answer.

10.2 The names of the backward pass

Each name holds rates: the change of the loss for each unit of change of an element of a forward value. It has the size of that forward value. A dd in front marks the gradient at a value that the forward pass computed, and a gg marks the gradient at a weight matrix.

  • dLdL: the gradient at the 27 scores of each row, before smsm. 16 by 27.

  • gLMgLM: the gradient at lmlm. 27 by 16.

  • dX4dX4: the gradient at x4x4. 16 by 16.

  • gF2gF2: the gradient at f2f2. 16 by 64.

  • dM0dM0: the gradient at m0m0. 16 by 64.

  • gF1gF1: the gradient at f1f1. 64 by 16.

  • dX3dX3: the gradient at x3x3. 16 by 16.

  • dX2dX2: the gradient at x2x2. 16 by 16.

  • gWOgWO: the gradient at wowo. 16 by 16.

  • dhdh: the gradient at the heads' averages, a vha vh. 4 planes of 16 by 4.

  • dVhdVh: the gradient at vhvh. 4 planes of 16 by 4.

  • dSdS: the gradient at the scores qh khTqh kh^T. 4 planes of 16 by 16.

  • dQhdQh, dKhdKh: the gradients at qhqh and khkh. 4 planes of 16 by 4 each.

  • dQdQ, dKdK, dVdV: the gradients at qq, kk and vv. 16 by 16 each.

  • gWQgWQ, gWKgWK, gWVgWV: the gradients at wqwq, wkwk and wvwv. 16 by 16 each.

  • dX1dX1: the gradient at x1x1. 16 by 16.

  • dXpdXp: the gradient at xpxp. 16 by 16.

  • dXdX: the gradient at xx. 16 by 16.

  • gWTEgWTE: the gradient at wtewte. 27 by 16.

  • gWPEgWPE: the gradient at wpewpe. 16 by 16.

The step returns lossloss and the nine gg matrices as one vector of 4,192 floats, in the layout of pp.

10.3 The terms

  • Token: an integer that stands for one character. 0 to 25 are a to z.

  • Mark, or BOS: the token 26. It stands before a name and after it.

  • Document: one name of the corpus.

  • Position: a place in a document's row of tokens, counted from 0. The model reads positions 0 to 15.

  • Row: one pair of a document and a position. Every matrix of the step has one row for each pair.

  • Batch: the documents that one step learns from.

  • Key, in Part 1: an integer that looks a row up in a table. idsids, tgtg and pospos are vectors of keys.

  • Parameter: one of the 4,192 floats that training changes.

  • Weight matrix: one of the nine matrices of parameters.

  • Vocabulary: the model's set of tokens, 27 of them. In the program's comments, "the array vocabulary" is the file FlatArrays.fss.

  • FlatArrays.fss: the file of array helpers that the program imports. It holds the functions and operators on vectors and matrices that Fortress's library does not have, such as rowsrows, gathergather and viewview.

  • rmsnrmsn: RMS norm, for root mean square. It divides a row by the root of the mean of its squares.

  • smsm: softmax. It turns a row of scores into probabilities.

  • View: a matrix or a vector without storage of its own. It reads and writes another array.

  • Plane: one of the matrices of an array with three indices.

  • Embedding: the row that a table holds for a token or for a position, a vector of 16 numbers.

  • Score: a number of any size and sign that goes into smsm.

  • Query, key and value, in attention: three vectors that each position makes from its row. A query with a key gives a score. The values are what gets averaged.

  • Head: one of the four attention computations, each on four of the sixteen columns.

  • Attention weight: a number between 0 and 1 that says how much one position reads from another. The model computes it anew in every forward pass.

  • Residual connection: adding a block's result to the block's input.

  • Forward pass: the part of the step from the tokens to the loss, lines 57 to 61.

  • Loss: one number that says how wrong the model's probabilities are for the right answers.

  • Gradient: for each parameter, the rate at which the loss changes when that parameter changes.

  • Backward pass: the part of the step that computes the gradient, lines 63 to 70.

  • Step: the function stepstep, one forward pass and one backward pass. A step of training is a step and one call of adamadam.
