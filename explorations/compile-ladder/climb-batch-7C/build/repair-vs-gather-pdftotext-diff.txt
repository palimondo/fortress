> ledger). It compares the declarations it sees by their names: for a parameterized T its verdict can depend on
> how an extender spells its static parameters, and a trait or object that extends a trait of the same simple name
> in another API counts as an extender (rows 489 and 490). The interpreter does not check comprises clauses.

> (Section 6.10, Section 24.4) and the definitions an abstract function declaration requires (Section 9.3), are not
> revised; whether they are to be restated at the level of values is not settled.
