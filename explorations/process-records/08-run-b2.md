## Findings

The run spent its first four minutes reading rather than running, and the reading was of the tree's own prior artifacts — the ledger, `matrix-ad-probes/REPORT.md`, `microgpt2.fss`, the blinded run's figures and goldens — before any Fortress file was opened for writing; the first probe it ran in the thread came at 01:14 and was a skeleton, not an exploration. Gate 2 was executed as a document with a stated blocking function: `inventory.md` names five mechanisms as unprobed and says the design "may not depend on a negative" until a worker settles them, and those five became the two gate-1 briefs eight minutes later. The three skeletons were built and rendered before the shape was chosen, and the choice is argued in `design.md` on immutability, on how the backward rules read, and on fan-out being a monoid sum, with the cost of skelC (a shared node's pullback re-entered once per use) stated rather than elided.

Time was lost in two places. The first is a chain of small spelling rejections in the skeletons and again in the program — nine distinct interpreter errors between 01:14 and 01:35, each costing an edit and a fifteen-second startup — most of which the ledger had already recorded or would have predicted (rows 7, 8, 30, 33). The second is the run's own first reading of `w |hs|` as a syntax error, which put parentheses into the program at 01:34 and was retired by the replication worker at 01:51; the workaround survived in the committed program for about twenty minutes and cost a re-render and a republish (commit d83626891). That is also the clearest instance of gate 4 doing what it was written for: the run's article had the claim, and an independent worker falsified it before it reached the reader.

The prior work in the tree was used as material rather than as a template. The blinded run's goldens were used as a cross-check on an independently derived set and not as a substitute; its `MicroGPTData.fss` was not reused, since the run generated its own fixture from its own derivation; and the article shows the blinded run's RMSNorm render beside the matrix-level one and records that the vector-level program reads closer for a per-vector formula. Delegation was consistent for probes from the first minute, but the gap table was initially going to be written in the thread and moved to a worker only after a mid-run reminder, which the thread then resolved by re-reading its own pre-compaction transcript instead of reconstructing the agreement from memory.

The effort divides roughly in half: about 7.7e5 output tokens and 6.7e7 context in the main thread against 1.1e5 and 5.5e7 across the five workers, for one verified program of about 200 lines, an article with fourteen rendered pairs, 39 gap rows and 100-odd probe files. The verification standard the brief set was met at machine epsilon rather than at the tolerance the run declared (1e-9), and the article says why: every sum runs in the same index order as the reference on one thread, and the tolerance is held for the day the reductions run in parallel.
