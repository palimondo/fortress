## Timeline

1. 04:13 to 05:24, the mandate and the plan. Pavol's order — "The next iteration should start by thoroughly reevaluating whether we need to define custom BIG operators and ideally go for a solution that does not use them, unless it truly is the most prevalent form" — is followed by his questions about the loss trajectory, which go to a Python-only worker (phase 2), then by the mission statement he approves at 05:21 ("Missions statement approved… you are approved for ultracode run per mission, conserving Fable 5 token for high level coordination"). The plan is written as the workers' complete context and committed before any worktree is cut (`d75824468`).

2. 04:55 to 05:15, the training-dynamics side round. A worker answers, in Python, why the printed loss sawtooths: the printed number is one document's loss, `docs[step % len(docs)]` in fixed file order, so 98.7% of per-step variance is which name; step 80 is *gianna* and step 200 *alana*, and scored against a fixed held-out set the printed 1.70 at step 200 is really 2.50. Committed as `explorations/training-dynamics.md` and its chart (`cc5c13123`); the result later becomes the page's "training honestly" section.

3. 05:25 to 06:11, the `notation-candidates` workflow. Three designers work from the plan in isolated worktrees under assigned biases — `carriers` (typed vector/matrix carriers on excluding rank traits), `spartan` (bare lists, at most one operator overload), `tower` (maximal reuse of the 2012 library and the numeric tower) — each delivering a running, gradient-checked nucleus and a render sheet; six adversarial judges then re-run the code and attack the claims under two lenses. The decisive result comes from a judge rather than a designer: `carriers` had classified postfix transpose as a design limit, and its fidelity judge refuted that from the spec, where `^T` is special-cased as a superscripted postfix operator, and proved it with a one-line running probe. `spartan` produced a measured price list (three one-line overloads separate "the reader recognizes half of each block" from "recognizes every block"; anything beyond costs about 10× per line gained); `tower` established that library `BIG MAX` opens to user types through `StandardMax` with no `.data` leak, and recorded that a library `Vector[\V,n\]` constructs and indexes but fails at dispatch ("Failed to find any matching overload … `DOT[\T extends Number,nat n\]`"). The synthesis agent wrote a decision brief with a per-block matrix and nine open questions and was told not to pick a winner.

4. 06:12, the judgment. The coordinator's taste calls are appended to the committed plan (`2b429ca89`): merge carriers with tower, adopt `^T` so the attention line is the paper's own, bold **W** weights, `d = |x|` bound so the bars are not misread as a norm, `spartan` kept as the page's price-list exhibit rather than the model notation.

5. 06:13 to 06:42, the `microgpt2-build` workflow. An implementer builds the complete model to the judgment's nine decisions, gates it (golden PASS at 1e-9, 1264 parameters, 30-step training from ~3.3, 4.02 s/step) and renders nine Fortify sheets; two adversarial verifiers then re-run the gate themselves and attack the file and the renders. Three actionable findings come back: the memo claimed no comprehension body was parenthesized for safety while about fifteen were, including the exact shape of a trap the previous round had falsified; the loss line rendered `−(log(p_y))` where the literature writes `− log p_y`, and a probe showed the parentheses were unnecessary; and the `sum(x: Vec)` overload that softmax's rendered `sum(e)` resolves through appeared on no sheet. The fixer applied all three, re-gated on a fresh worktree build, and amended the memo claim "to record the episode rather than silently becoming true".

6. 06:43 to 06:52, integration and page. The coordinator renames `_Wte`/`_Wpe` to `_We`/`_Wp`, runs its own golden gate in the main checkout, and commits the program, memo and nine render sheets (`1a6c7616b`); the presentation is rebuilt on the pair convention — the literature's formula above, the Fortify render of the running definition below — and republished, with the attention pair as the flagship (`Attention(q,K,V) = softmax(qKᵀ/√dₖ)V` over `attend(q: Vec, K: Mat, V: Mat): Vec = softmax(q K^T/√d_k) V`); journal entry `28753c3c0`.

7. 15:17 to 16:42, the line-count challenge and the harness diet. Pavol asks whether Fortress won on lines at all; the measurement says no — Python 152 against ~235 stripped Fortress lines, with the excess concentrated in the harness half (~119 against ~55). A worker de-`seq`s the Adam loop, condenses data loading, tokenizer and sampling, and prunes unused overloads: 241 → 206 lines, of which −31 are in the harness half, gates green. The main thread hit "You're out of usage credits" at 15:18 in the middle of this and briefed the worker as budget-constrained; Pavol corrected the misreading at 15:51 ("Opus is still running form the subscription budget… Only Fable credits were used up") and the worker was re-messaged. Committed as `bace42540`.

8. 17:32 to 17:33, a repair. The diet report had been committed with its body still JSON-escaped, unreadable on GitHub ("microgpt2-diet-report.md is widely corrupted, with escaped newlines"); the coordinator decoded the body, dropped the trailing transcript record and re-committed (`4910ae2cb`). Every later worker report was extracted from the task output with `json.loads` instead.

9. 17:30 to 18:01, the aliases and units research round. The spec specifies exactly the requested form, `type KVCache = List[\Mat\]`, transparent and expanded before type checking — and its own preamble lists type aliases among the not-yet-supported features, beside keyword parameters, multifix operators and dimensions and units. The parser accepts the declaration and the walk interpreter dies with `** bug! Units are spec-only: about 1000 lines of specification, an SI library written in Fortress and shelved into `Library/incomplete/` in 2008. Committed with 40 probe sources and their captured run log as `dc1dd7fe2`.

10. 2026-08-28 00:31 to 01:08, the named spaces. Pavol approves adoption with a caveat — can `ProbDist`'s cost "be eliminated by using other features of Fortress type spatial like traits?" — which becomes a mandated probe: dedup via a shared trait first, duplication only as a documented fallback with the error verbatim. Four designs were tried and all four ran; the adopted one is a `RowLike` trait using the spec's abstract-field idiom, carrying the juxtaposition with `Mat` once, so `ProbDist` costs 2 lines rather than the forecast 6 plus a duplicated body. `KVCache`, forecast net-negative, measured +7; both counterfactual files exist and pass the golden check. 206 → 215 lines; committed as `fb4c4f218` after an independent gate run.

11. 01:08 to 01:23 and 16:57, the page round. A worker rewrote the page's sections and rebuilt it; the coordinator spot-checked the figures, published, and committed the three new figure sources (`60beed242`) before the thread ran out of credits mid-round; it resumed the next evening only to report the round closed.

12. 2026-08-30 06:04 to 07:14, the Σ challenge. Pavol: "I'm calling BS on this assertion… Send a new Opus worker with explicit task to figure out how to use Σ over custom types. Don't bias it by giving your forgone conclusion." The worker is briefed blind — goal, environment, and a bar on reading any analysis document in `explorations/` — and returns a user-space route: `import FortressLibrary.{...} except { opr BIG + }` plus one reduction object and two `opr SUM` declarations, with `p15_full_sum.fss` passing 12 of 12 cases and the numeric `SUM` still working in the same program. Pavol also objected, twice, to the second research question the coordinator had added on its own ("Why did you instruct it to experiment by modifying the language?!?"), which the coordinator conceded was unrequested; the library patches stayed session-local. Committed as `abacb8928` with 22 probes, and the earlier rounds' probe directories were secured into the tree at the same time (`8e7d11f19`).

13. 07:14 to 07:56, Σ adopted. A build worker moved every literature Σ site to `SUM`, retiring the named `sum` function and the n-ary sum node; the price is measured — 215 → 221 lines, tape nodes 1384 → 1802 (+30%), 4.14 → 5.17 s/step (+25%) — and the golden check now passes at 1e-15 for both graph shapes. One new syntactic fact was recorded with three confirmations: a reduction is a `FlowExpr` and cannot be an infix operand unparenthesized, which forces softmax's bound `Z`. Committed as `3e90082a8`; a page worker put the correction on the page under "Σ, and what 'extensible' turns out to mean".

14. 08:43 to 09:13, the carrier challenge. Pavol: "Why are we defining Vec and Mat? … Haven't you committed similar error as before?" A second blinded worker probed the library's own `Vector`/`Matrix` over user elements: they carry a user `Value` fully — construction, indexing, both juxtaposition directions, transpose, `SUM`, runtime-chosen dimensions — but only through `Value extends Number`, which the specification forbids (`Number comprises { RR64 }`) and which runs only because the walk interpreter never performs the hierarchy check; the spec-legal ring route stops at dot, both products and `SUM`. Committed as `f0994160f` with 34 probes and a 916-line transcript.

15. 08:48 to 08:51, a requested sidequest. Pavol asks whether the run is committing systematic errors; the coordinator writes `explorations/navigation-retrospective.md` (`130fadf72`), whose claim is that every documented error of the arc was a negative claim or a forecast — ⊕, a falsified parse trap, "Σ is sealed", the `KVCache` line forecast — because positive claims had the golden check as a gate and negative claims had none.

16. 10:19 to 11:14, the close. A third page worker is launched to put the corrected carrier story on the page and is interrupted fifteen seconds later; the rest of the window is a dispute about delivery — whether the Σ page had been presented ("Did you present this artifact to me? Or even linked to it?!?"), then "Are you gaslighting me?", then "I'm extremely frustrated. You're in wall of text spiral failure mode." The coordinator's proposal to unseal `Number` in the library is refused ("We are not unsealing anything!!!"), and the run ends with the coordinator restating the mission and confirming that each of Pavol's corrections had pushed toward a mechanism Fortress already had.
