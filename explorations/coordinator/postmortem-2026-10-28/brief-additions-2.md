# Two sentences after the pilot, as exact edits

Not applied. Each file is under `explorations/coordinator/tools/record-clarity/`. Each anchor occurs once in its file.

The cause: the findings sub-bullet says to correct a sentence "from its ground, opened at its source", and the pilot's writer wrote the ground into the entry. P-37 grew from 1,056 to 1,776 characters and P-43 from 662 to 1,453 (`explorations/reviews/foundations/evidence/corrections/p/checks.md`, section 3). `record.md`, "What an entry is", sends a date to the history (question 3), keeps a count in one entry that the others name (its count rule), and leaves line numbers and lists of sites in the report (question 4).

## 1. `writer.md`, the findings sub-bullet

After the sentence "Correct each sentence that a finding names from its ground, opened at its source: there, and only there, the meaning changes.", in the same sub-bullet:

> State the corrected reading as the decision and its reason, and point to the FACTS entry or the report section that holds its ground; the ground's dates, counts, commits and file names go in the apply file's note line, not in the entry.

## 2. `check.md`, the checker's clause for POSITIONS

After the sentence "A change that no finding names is one, and so is a changed decision, growth beyond what the corrections need, or a sentence that tells how the decision came about.", in the same bullet:

> So is a new sentence that carries a date, a count, a commit or a file path, unless it is a pointer to an entry or a report section.

The checker's exception names a report section as well as an entry. Without it, the checker would fail the pointer that sentence 1 tells the writer to write.
