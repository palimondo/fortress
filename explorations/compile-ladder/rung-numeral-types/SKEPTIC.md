## The contested fix

- The worker's argument (`REPORT.md`, "Decisions", 6): "The return conversion in `FunctionClosure` and `MethodClosure`, at a declared return type only; elsewhere the default. Walk checks no return type, so a numeral that does not convert there is its default."
- Mine: the section says "A numeral out of range at a typed place raises there when it runs", and fork N7, the record's default for walk's places, names the return among them and says what happens where the numeral does not convert: "At a chosen declaration's parameter, a typed binding, a field and a declared return type, the checker's places, a numeral converts from its exact value: into the eight number types ..., a numeral out of the type's range refused at the place when it runs, as walk refuses one at a binding today (row 325's walk half); into a numeral type as the numeral object; into a program's type through its coercion from the numeral type ...; else refused" (`explorations/coordinator/CLIMB-BATCH-29.md:355`). As built, `wide(): ZZ32 = 3000000000` answered a `ZZ64`, and the catch of every `FortressException` turned a program's raising coercion into the numeral's default; the binding, the field and the parameter refuse or raise in each case.
