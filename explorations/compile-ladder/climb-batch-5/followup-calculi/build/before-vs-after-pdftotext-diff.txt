> the shape it admits that the rule refuses: in Basic Core Fortress, an object that extends two different instantiations
> of a parameterized trait declaring no method, so that its statement that all of its valid programs are valid Fortress

> Rationale The calculi are the team’s formal record of this working draft as it stood, and the rule changes the language,
> not that record. New premises in their rules would be new formal work, and would reopen soundness claims
> that were proved for the rules as they are written. The revival decided on this treatment on 26 September 2026.
> Effect None on the language or on either implementation. The three calculi admit programs, in the shapes named,
> that are not valid Fortress programs.
