## Findings

The run front-loaded the two things its brief made gates and did them in that order: it pinned the reference and derived its own goldens before writing Fortress (`tools/derive_checks.py` at 17:04, goldens at 17:05), and it rendered candidate spellings at 17:07 and 17:10, before the first line of the program existed, so the type-spelling question (`Value[n]`, `Value^n`, `Indexed⟦Value,ℤ32⟧`) was settled by looking at figures rather than by argument.

It spent its first working hour on the language rather than on the model: 39 probes, of which the `probe02` family alone is ten files chasing one question, `∑` over a user type, from an invalid overload through an interpreter leniency it refused to use, through a differently named big operator, to the specification's `except`-import; that route is what the primary program ships.

The design work is organized as one verified baseline plus alternatives measured against it: v1 passed the 52 checks at 17:35 and every later formulation had to pass the same file, which is why v2 and v3 each cost about ten minutes of error-chasing and then a check run, and why three complete programs exist instead of one.

Most of the errors it hit are surface-level interpreter and grammar facts — call-then-subscript, subscript-then-`^`, keyword and all-uppercase names, `var` in an api, getters before methods, a `do` block in a comprehension — and each was fixed in place within a turn or two, usually by parenthesizing or renaming; the run isolated three of them into standalone probes (`probe17_subexp`, `probe07`, `probe02*`) and left the rest recorded in the notes.

Where a first error could have become a negative claim, it kept going: the object varargs rejection produced a varargs *factory*, the `T[n]` failure produced `Array1`/`Array2` with explicit bounds and later a `Vec` carrier, and the `except { SUM }` parse error produced four more spellings until one parsed.

Time was lost in two identifiable places: the setup's missing Emacs cost about six minutes of waiting plus the two-turn stop-and-report protocol, and the article's SVG glyph-id collisions and dark-mode check cost the last ten minutes (18:06–18:16), including four screenshot inspections of the same page.

It had no prior work in the tree to use — the brief removed `explorations/`, `research/` and `CLAUDE.md` from the checkout — and its one exposure was a link in the root `README.md`, which it reported rather than followed.

The effort bought a program of 267 lines with two complete alternative formulations, an article whose every Fortress figure is typeset from the source that ran, and a 15-row departure table; the assessment it wrote itself names what the specimen does not establish (the compiler path, the typechecker, and the parallelism claim for the sequence form, all unexercised).
