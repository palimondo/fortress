## 1. How the practice came about, step by step

The practice has these parts: (a) a timing carries its machine, and only a pair taken in one run measures a difference; (b) the microGPT walk check runs once per batch on the landed tree; (c) it is quick, under a minute; (d) its seconds are printed and quoted; (e) reviews compare them with the batch before; (f) a timed pair of old and new code is proposed when they rise. For each step: the time, the commit, the text, who wrote it, the coordinator's words, and his message before it.
