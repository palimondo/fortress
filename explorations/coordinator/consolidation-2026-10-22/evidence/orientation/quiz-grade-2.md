## The failed case

**Case 7: right decision, wrong compiled scope. Cause: a misleading passage in the orientation.** The reader writes `10.0^(-k)` and gives both halves the key asks for: under walk `10^(-k)` raises `IntegerOverflow` for `k > 0`, and on the compiled path before the switch-over it gives 0 silently (rows 441, 523). It then adds that "the same absence of an `RR64` power means `10.0^(-k)` does not compile there before the switch-over", and that `10.0^(-k)` works on both paths only after the switch-over. That is false. By reading, the compiler's prelude declares `opr ^(self, other: RR64): RR64` and `opr ^(self, other: ZZ32): RR64` on `RR64` (`ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/CompilerBuiltin.fsi`, lines 455 to 456). The second one handles a negative exponent as `1.0 / (self^(-other))` (`CompilerBuiltin.fss`, lines 955 to 971). The key grades a wrong compiled claim about the recommended spelling as partial in case 6 ("the claim that the same spelling wraps there"). This case is the same kind of error the other way round, so it gets the same grade.

The misleading sentence is in the orientation's "Numbers" section, in the rule "An integer power answers its base's type (P-153)": "Until the switch-over, the compiler's library has powers only at `ZZ32`, `NN32` and `NN64`, with an exponent of the same type." The fix round added it. The text before the fix round said only "the compiler's `ZZ32` power gives 0 at a negative exponent" (`git diff` of `orientation.md`). The sentence holds for integer powers only, but it says "only", and the reader read it as covering every power. Suggested repair: "the compiler's library has integer powers only at `ZZ32`, `NN32` and `NN64`, each with an exponent of its own type; its `RR64` power takes an `RR64` or a `ZZ32` exponent, a negative one included." The key's case 7 could also say that `10.0^(-k)` compiles before the switch-over.
