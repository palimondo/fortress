## FACTS.md

Under "Landed semantics", after "The one library's number tower is flat":

- **`RR32` is a sibling of `RR64` under `Number`, and `RR64` converts from it** (2026-09-29, climb batch 6.5b rung V, `e455ccd98`; `compile-ladder/rung-rr32-sibling/`; rows 435, 528-531).
  - **The declarations.** `value object RR32 extends { Number, StandardPartialOrder[\RR32\], StandardMinMax[\RR32\], AdditiveGroup[\RR32\], MultiplicativeRing[\RR32\] }`, `RR64`'s algebra at its own type (`ProjectFortress/LibraryBuiltin/FortressBuiltin.fsi:47-48`, `.fss:203-204`). `Number comprises { RR64, RR32, QQ, AnyIntegral }`. `RR64 excludes { QQ, AnyIntegral, RR32 } comprises { Float, FloatLiteral }`, with `coerce(x: RR32) = asFloat(x)` beside its coercion from `ZZ32` (`Library/FortressLibrary.fsi:282`, `:291-296`, `.fss:359`, `:387-392`). `RR32`'s api declares the operators its component defines, as `NN32`'s and `RR64`'s do (`FortressBuiltin.fsi:69-116`). Its 30 binary natives take `b:RR32`, the argument they read (row 435's second fix), and its `MIN`, `MAX` and `MINMAX` answer at `RR32`. Its integer power is `narrow((asFloat(self))^b)` (`FortressBuiltin.fss:328`). `Number`'s `=` treats an `RR32` as a float (`Library/FortressLibrary.fss:367`, `:370`).
  - **What walk answers.** Under `walk`, an `RR32` is not an `RR64` in a `typecase`. Two `RR32` values combine into an `RR32` by `RR32`'s own operators, `/` and `SQRT` among them. An `RR32` with an `RR64`, a float numeral or a `ZZ32` converts to `RR64` and answers an `RR64`, as the specification's example computes (`Specification/basic/conversions-coercions.tex:904-912`). An `RR32` bound to an `RR64` variable or parameter converts. A generic `T` over an `RR32` and an `RR64` is `RR64` by answer 8's promotion. `=`, `<` and `CMP` with an `RR64` answer. Nothing coerces into `RR32`, and `narrow` stays explicit.
  - **The specification.** `Specification/basic-lib/numbers.tex:27-56` names `ℝ32` among the number types and `ℝ64`'s coercion from it, with a callout. Appendix I's entry "The single-precision floating-point type" (`Specification/appendices/changes.tex:1714-1790`) revises batch 6's sentence that `ℝ32` stays below `ℝ64` (`:485-487`); the reasoning is in `compile-ladder/rung-rr32-sibling/decision-record.md`.
