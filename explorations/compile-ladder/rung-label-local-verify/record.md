## FACTS

**The code generator keeps a local variable in its environment for the rest of its block only, so a closure or task after an `if` arm or another nested block reads no slot of the block's bindings.** `CodeGen.forLocalVarDecl` adds a declaration's bindings to the environment of its body, after the initializer (`compiler/codegen/CodeGen.java:4812-4818`); before, a binding first in an arm stayed in the enclosing block's environment, and a later local function, function expression or forked call whose body named a variable of that name, its own binding or a numeral's coercion parameter `r` (row 983), captured the arm's slot, unset on the path that skipped the arm: 'VerifyError: Bad local variable type' as the class loaded (row 1078, the one library's `simplestRationalBetween`). With it, the planner's rehearsal tree runs `Hello` against the one library with the verifier on, and ASM's verifier refuses none of `FortressLibrary`'s 4,694 methods. Test: `compiler_tests/BlockBindingScopeCompiled`. Detail: `compile-ladder/rung-label-local-verify/REPORT.md`, "The cause, by the bytecode" and "The repair in the scratch copy".
