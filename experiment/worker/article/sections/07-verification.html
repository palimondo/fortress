7. Verification against the reference, and cost

Reference. Karpathy's microgpt.py gist (8627fe009c40f57531cb18360106ce95), revision 14fb038816c7aae0bb9342c2dbf1a51dd134a5ff, 199 lines, sha256 d47d88c2fd432c8ebdc1048beab7f7eb64ea7e0e664e11b812d72a6d95ebccee, with the names dataset it pins (makemore names.txt at commit 988aa59, 32 032 names). The article and the gist agree on all model, autograd, optimizer and inference code; the gist additionally seeds the random generator (42), which the checks rely on.

Check data. A harness (tools/derive_checks.py) executes the pinned source verbatim up to the optimizer section — seed, data shuffle, tokenizer, Value, parameter initialization, model — and then runs an arithmetically identical, instrumented copy of the training loop for two steps and of the inference loop for three samples, recording: the initial weights; per position the logits, probabilities and loss; the total loss; every gradient; the Adam-updated parameters; and, for sampling, the per-step probabilities, the uniform draws consumed by random.choices and the chosen tokens. Its two losses, 3.365967 and 3.424273, are the values printed in the microgpt article for steps 1 and 2. The data is exported as a generated Fortress component (src/MicroGPTData.fss, decimal literals that round-trip exactly).

Checks. The check program builds the model from the reference's initial weights and, for each step, compares 27 logits at every position, the per-position losses and the loss, then runs backward and Adam and compares all 4 192 gradients and updated parameters matrix by matrix; then it samples three names replaying the recorded draws and compares probabilities, tokens and text. Tolerance: 10-12 absolute, four orders of magnitude above the largest observed difference and four below the smallest quantity of interest (gradients are O(10-3–10-1), Adam updates O(10-2)). The only source of difference is summation order: Python's sum is a left fold, a Fortress ∑ combines terms in the order its generator chooses, which perturbs a 16–64-term dot product at the 10-16 level.
