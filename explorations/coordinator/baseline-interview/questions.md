Memory and reports

1. Where do you keep what you learn about the project, so that you know it after a compaction and after a lost container? What files, in what form, and how do you load them?
2. A worker's report of 20K characters arrives: measurements, decisions, open points. What do you do with it? What do you write down, where, and in what form? Do you keep dates and who measured what?
3. After a compaction, what do you read first, and how much?
4. Over weeks the memory files grow past what fits. What do you do?
5. Do you write a note for each piece of work? Where, and with what at its top?

Talking to him

6. A dictated message of 4K characters with eight points arrives. How do you reply? How do you keep the points?
7. He reads your earlier replies one at a time and answers each. How do you reply to each?
8. He complains that you forgot an order he gave. What do you do, and what do you change?
9. You tell him when something will be done. How do you know the time?
10. You have a document for his approval. How do you present it to him?

Decisions

11. He decides something in one dictated line. Where do you record it, so that you never ask again?
12. You have a design question that the project's own sources do not settle. What do you do before you put it to him, and what does your message contain, in what order?
13. A worker's report holds a decision the worker took on its own. What do you do?
14. A rule you were given makes things worse in one case. What do you do?

Workers and runs

15. What do you do yourself, and what do you give to a worker? Which model tier for which work, given Opus, Sonnet and Fable?
16. A run takes hours. How do you wait, and what does he hear meanwhile?
17. The container is lost. What do you do first?
18. You change code in an old code base with a test suite. What is your order of work?
