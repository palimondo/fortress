  - `generate` 4: the checker's capture defect, which the triage cleared by renaming a type parameter (cited). It is not a Meet Rule error.
  - `juxtaposition` 1: `String`'s own pair. Its meet `(String, String)` is declared, but the two functional methods' `self` sit at different positions, which the specification refuses outright and the checker's meet rule enforces (triage section 4: `advanced/overloading.tex:436-441`; `OverloadingChecker.scala:509`, `fsp == gsp`). A marker device as for the range kinds would apply. Batch 7R's scalar ranges over `ZZ32` remove the parameter first. Not measured.

## 2. The devices and what each clears

Two runs:
- `P2/devices.py` with its default letters `KGFMQCS`: 87 → 48 (`count/table-sketch-p2.txt`, `errors-sketch-p2.txt`).
- With `KGMQCSR`: 47 (`count/table-sketch-p2r.txt`).

The per-family counts are from `famcount.py` over the error lists.

- **K, the five range kinds.** Each of `OpenRange`, `ExtentRange`, `LeftRange`, `RightRange` and `FullRange` extends a plain marker, and the five markers exclude one another. This is the device of `Rank1` to `Rank3` ("Potemkin exclusion traits", `Library/FortressLibrary.fsi`) and of `AnyMaybe`. `FORWARD_CMP` goes 19 → 0, and `IN`'s one pair between two kinds (`OpenRange` against `ExtentRange`) goes.
- **G, ranges against generators.** `Range` extends a marker `AnyRange`, which `Condition` excludes. `PartialRange` extends `AnyPartialRange`, which `Generator` excludes. A range is never a `Condition`, and a partial range is never a `Generator`. It clears 5 `IN` pairs: `Condition`'s against `Range`'s, `OpenRange`'s and `ExtentRange`'s, and `Generator`'s against `OpenRange`'s and `ExtentRange`'s.
- **F, a meet declaration on `FullRange`.** `opr IN(n: I, self)`, since `FullRange` is both a `Range` and a `Generator`. It does not clear `Generator`'s `IN` against `Range`'s. `FullRange` is below both, but it does not cover every type that could be both.
- **R, instead of F.** `Range`'s abstract `IN` is declared on `PartialRange` instead. A full range then takes `IN` from `Generator` and a partial range from `PartialRange`, which G makes exclude `Generator`. This clears that last `FortressLibrary` `IN`, 48 → 47, with no new error on this stage. Whether a body calls `IN` on a value typed `Range[\I\]` would show on the distance stage, which was not run for it.
- **M, `Maybe`'s `map` and `ivmap`** declared on `Maybe`, the meet of `Condition` and `Indexed` (the triage's T3 lines). `ivmap` 3 → 0, and `map` 4 → 2. The two left are the scalar sequential ranges (above).
- **Q, `SQCAP` on `Just` at `(Just, Maybe)`**, the meet of `Maybe`'s and `Just`'s own: 1 → 0.
- **C, `copy` redeclared on `StandardMutableArrayType`**, the array diamond's meet (the triage's T2 line; rung A's place for `tabulate` and `fill`): 1 → 0.
- **S, the triage's two slips.** `AssociativeReduction.lift(r:R)` and `LexicographicReduction.isLeftZero(_:TotalComparison)`: `lift` 6 → 0, `isLeftZero` 1 → 0. They are declared-type slips, listed with batch 7's "left out" one-line slips.

Outside the class, nothing moved. The other 39 errors are `CAP` 12, `seq` 5, the array `MIN` and `MAX` 8, juxtaposition against the ring 3, `openRangeHelper` 3, and the return-type slips `CMP` 2, `atMost` 2, `shift` 2, `every` 1, `unsigned` 1. They read the same before and after.
