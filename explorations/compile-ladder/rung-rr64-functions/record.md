## Two observations for the coordinator, neither a ledger row

**The brief's spec citation for the real-numbers section does not exist.** `CLIMB-BATCH-1.md` section F says "the 'Real' numbers section of that chapter is commented out (`numbers.tex:920`)". `Specification/basic-lib/numbers.tex` is 566 lines long and has exactly one section, "Rational Numbers" (`:12-17`); there is no real-numbers section, commented out or otherwise, and no file under `Specification/basic-lib` or `Specification/advanced-lib` reaches line 920 except `advanced-lib/binary.tex`. The chapter's many `%` lines are the ASCII sources of the typeset displays, which `map/design-intent-sources.md:104` already records for `algebraic-constraints.tex`. The conclusion the brief drew is right — the specification has no chapter for the real types — but because the chapter was never written, not because it was switched off.

**The `(api listing)` citations of rungs 4, 5 and 7 point at paths that are not in the tree.** Gap ledger rows 314 and 316 cite `Specification/library/apis/FortressLibrary.tex` and `…/CompilerLibrary.tex`, and the comment at `ProjectFortress/library_tests/IntLiteralArithRung7.fss:30-31` cites `…/CompilerBuiltin.tex`. `Specification/library/apis/` does not exist: those files are `\input` files generated from the `.fsi` sources when the specification is built (`Specification/library/default-libraries.tex:27,39`), and the two `Compiler*` ones are not even among the inputs the spec build names. So such a citation is not merely circular in the sense of `Specification/library/structure.tex:25-31`; it cannot be opened. Worth a narrowing of the convention in the next brief, and worth knowing that rung 7's citation is inside a source file, where `protocol.md` §2 says provenance does not belong.
